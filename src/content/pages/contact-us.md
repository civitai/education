Questions, problems or ideas? Here's how to reach us.

- **Help with your account or a purchase:** open a ticket at [civitai.com/support](https://civitai.com/support), or email [support@civitai.com](mailto:support@civitai.com).
- **General inquiries:** [hello@civitai.com](mailto:hello@civitai.com)
- **Chat with the community and staff:** [discord.gg/civitai](https://discord.gg/civitai)
- **Found a mistake in a guide?** Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/civitai) or at [civitai.com/support](https://civitai.com/support).
