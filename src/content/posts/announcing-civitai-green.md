Civitai has long been recognized as a vibrant hub for AI enthusiasts, artists, and developers. Our platform has always been about fostering creativity and enabling individuals to push the boundaries of what’s possible with AI. To better serve our diverse community, we are in the process of restructuring the platform into two distinct sections:

**[Civitai.com](https://www.civitai.com):** **This section will remain unchanged**. A playground for artistic exploration, where users can experiment with AI-driven tools and share their innovative projects with a like-minded community. We believe in the power of creativity to drive technological advancement, and this space will remain dedicated to those who seek to challenge the status quo and explore the limitless potential of AI.

**[Civitai Green](https://civitai.green/):** In recognition of the growing demand for a more controlled area for content engagement, we are launching a new section that will cater to users looking for a more secure and straightforward experience. This space is designed to facilitate commercial activities while ensuring that all interactions are conducted within a safe and respectful framework. **Our goal is to provide a platform that supports both creativity and commerce, with robust safeguards in place to protect all participants.**

Civitai Green is intended to be as seamless an experience for users as possible:

-   Having or creating an account on [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) will also create an account on [Civitai.green](http://civitai.green/) and vice versa

-   While logged in any content or resources posted on [Civitai.Green](http://civitai.green/) will also post to [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/), while only safe resource posted to [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) will appear on [Civitai.green](http://civitai.green/)

-   Currently Some features of [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) are not available on [Civitai.Green](http://civitai.green/) until we can guarantee the safety of the content.
