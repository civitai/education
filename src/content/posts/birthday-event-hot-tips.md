Civitai turns 4, and the whole site is playing. Four color teams compete from November 1 until the event ends on **December 1 at 00:00 UTC**, so the last scoring day is November 30. You score for your team with party hats, and attention is what counts: views, reactions, comments, stickers, remixes and stories. Buzz you spend does not score. This guide covers the whole event, step by step, and it stops being useful when the event ends.

The official sources are the [event page](https://civitai.com/events/birthday2026) and the [Hat Turf article](https://civitai.com/articles/38205/hat-turf-stick-your-teams-hats-on-other-peoples-images). If anything here ever disagrees with them, they win.

## Step 1: Join a team {#join}

1. Open the [event page](https://civitai.com/events/birthday2026) and press **Join a team** (or **Join and get your free hat**).
2. You land on one of four teams at random: Pink, Green, Blue or Yellow. Teams are final, so there is no switching. The event spreads the most active creators evenly across the four teams so nobody starts out stacked.
3. You get a free **Party Cap** in your team's color. It is all you need to play every part of the event.

The event page also shows live team standings, the hardest-working hats and the top Hat Turf players. When the event ends, every member of the **winning** team whose hats earned points gets the animated **Birthday 2026 Champion** badge in the winning color.

## Step 2: Wear your hat {#wear-your-hat}

A hat sits on one of **your own** images, models or articles, and the attention that item gets scores for your team. Anyone's views, reactions and comments count, as long as they are signed in and not you. Your own actions on your own hats earn nothing.

- **Images:** an image has to be in a post before it can wear a hat. Images score views, reactions, comments, approved stickers, accepted remixes and stories.
- **Models:** models score views and thumbs-up reviews.
- **Articles:** articles score views, reactions and comments.

Each hat sits on one thing at a time. You can move it whenever you like, with a 10-minute wait between moves. Hats keep earning while you are away, so there is nothing you must do every day.

### How scoring works {#scoring}

Different actions are worth different amounts, and the event page lists the current values. These rules apply to everything:

- Most actions count once per person per item. Many different people beat one fan clicking everything.
- Your own views and reactions on your own work do not count.
- Only people who are signed in count. Actions from brand-new accounts made just before the event, and from banned accounts, do not count.
- Only actions before the event ends count.

### Strategies for your own hat {#hat-strategies}

**Put the hat on your best item.** A hat earns only on the exact thing it sits on, so pick what people will actually open. A strong cover or first image is worth the hat.

**Move it to what is working.** Check which of your items are getting views and re-place your hat there. Remember the 10-minute wait, so do not bounce it around.

**Hat something people want to remix, then accept fast.** An accepted remix is the biggest single payout for an image, and it only counts after you accept it. Nothing scores while a submission sits in your queue, so check it daily. Open your user menu, then **Placements**, switch to the **Remixes** tab and open **Received**, or go straight to [civitai.com/user/placements?type=remix&tab=received](https://civitai.com/user/placements?type=remix&tab=received).

**Hat your own remix post too.** A remix shows up in the original image's gallery and is also your own post. Place a hat on that post's image and it earns for you as well.

**Hat a model and ask for reviews.** Models keep getting visitors for days, so a hat on a model keeps paying after you log off. A thumbs-up review counts only while the reviewer still recommends the model, so ask people who actually use it.

**Reach many different people.** Post, share the link, and ask real people to take a look.

## Step 3: Team up on teammates' work {#teammates}

Teammates are your easiest crowd, and they are playing the same game.

- **React and comment** on a teammate's hatted image. A thoughtful comment helps the creator too, so make it a real one.
- **Add a regular sticker** if you have one. It scores once the image's owner approves it. Read more about stickers in [Civitai's Guide to the Creator Shop and Stickers](/civitais-guide-to-the-creator-shop-and-stickers/).
- **Remix for free.** When you and the image owner are on the same team and the image owner is wearing their own team hat on it, your remix submission is free, does not use up your daily free placement, and you get several a day. The submit card shows how many are left today. To do it, remix their image with the on-site generator and post the result. In the post editor a remix card appears under the image, and you submit it to the original's Remix gallery from there. The team waiver is applied for you. The owner still reviews it, and it only counts once they accept.
- **Rally your color.** Start a collection of your team's hatted work, or share it in a group or chat, and say which image is wearing your hat. Ask for real engagement from real people. Never trade reactions.

For another team's image, the ordinary free placement applies instead: one a day, shared between stickers and remix galleries.

## Step 4: Play Hat Turf {#hat-turf}

Team Hats is about your own work. **Hat Turf** is how you play on everyone else's. You stick your team's hat on images and videos you like, for free, and the team with the most people on an image leads it. While your team leads, the earliest few players on it earn points from its views, reactions, comments, stickers and remixes. Hat Turf was built so that people who mostly browse can score for their team too.

Think of a hat you place on someone else's work as a **turf hat**, to keep it separate from the hat you wear on your own work.

If you have not joined Hat Turf, nothing changes for you: you will not see turf hats, badges or counts on any image.

### How to play {#turf-how-to}

1. **Join Hat Turf.** Tap **Join** on any image, or on the event page. You need to be on a team first. Playing goes both ways: while you are in, your own images are in too.
2. **Place your turf hat.** Tap the hat button on any image or video, or right from the feed. Drag the hat where you want it, then save. It costs no Buzz, and every design earns the same, so your free Party Cap works just as well as a hat you bought.
3. **Claim the image.** The team with the most people on an image leads it. While your team leads, the earliest few players on that team earn from it. This applies to Hat Turf only, not to wearing hats on your own work. Accounts created before the event started earn right away. Accounts created after it started earn once they reach the Spark rank on their Creator Journey.

### The rules {#turf-rules}

- **One turf hat per person per image.** Make it count. Once it is on, you cannot move it, and there is no refund.
- **Images and videos only.** Anything in a visible post. Not models, articles or contest entries.
- **Most people wins.** One turf hat each means nobody can carry an image alone, so bring your team. Ties go to whoever got there first.
- **King of the hill.** If another team takes the lead, they earn from then on. What your team already earned stays.
- **Regular stickers are unchanged.** Stickers and remixes still cost what the owner set, and the owner still gets that Buzz.
- **A worn hat mutes turf.** If the owner is wearing a hat on an image, turf hats can still go on, but they do not earn until that hat comes off.
- **Your own views do not count.**

Your supply of turf hats does not depend on how many hats you own. Fresh ones arrive every few minutes and you can save some up, so a day away does not cost you much. Teams that are behind get them faster and can hold more, which keeps the race close. Your current count is in the Hat Turf panel on any image, and the event page shows how fast you refill.

### Reading the turf badge {#turf-badge}

Every image and video in Hat Turf shows a turf badge, in the feeds and on the image page. It updates live, so you can watch a fight happen. The badge shows how the hats on the image split between teams, your team's position (how many hats ahead or behind), and a lock when the owner's hat is protecting the image. While the lock is on, the team that would lead is held back.

If you would rather not see turf hats on the pictures, turn off **Show hats** in the Hat Turf panel on an image. You still play, and the badge and numbers stay. It is set per device.

### Strategies for Hat Turf {#turf-strategies}

**Find rising work early.** The team with the most turf hats on an image earns from it, and only that team's earliest players get credit. Getting your hat onto a rising creator's work before the crowd arrives is the best move in the game.

**Bring your team.** A lead can flip. Tell teammates which image you are claiming so they stack on, and watch the badge to see when another team is closing in.

**Do not waste a hat on a protected image.** If the badge shows the lock, the owner is wearing a hat there and turf hats will not earn until it comes off.

**Claim your own images first.** Your own images and videos are fair game for other teams. Place a turf hat on yours and it starts out as your team's turf, and one player from another team cannot flip it alone.

**Decide between protecting and earning.** A hat you wear on your own image protects it: nobody's turf hats score there while it is on, so it scores for you alone. Leaving it open to turf can earn more for your team if people pile on. Protect your most popular work, and leave the rest open.

**Spend your turf hats.** Because they refill, a full supply is wasted. Use them, especially if your team is behind and refills faster.

### Keeping turf off your images {#turf-off}

If you do not want other people playing on your images, first leave Hat Turf (from the Hat Turf section of the event page or your account settings), then turn off **Let people play Hat Turf on my images**. The switch stays greyed out while you are joined. With it off, the turf hats on your images are hidden and stop counting, and they come back if you turn it on again. Playing goes both ways, so you cannot place turf hats while it is off. Turf hats you already placed on other people's images keep counting after you leave.

## Why isn't my turf earning? {#turf-troubleshooting}

- Your team may not be leading the image.
- You may not be among the earliest few on the leading team. You still count toward your team's lead.
- The owner may be wearing a hat on it.
- Your account may have been created after the event started and not yet reached the Spark rank.

## Play it straight {#fair-play}

Fine: asking real people to look, react, comment, sticker and remix, and sharing your team's collections and posts. Not fine: extra accounts, bought or bot engagement, reaction rings of throwaway accounts, and mass low-effort reposts made only to carry hats. Those can be removed from the event.

## After the event {#after}

You keep every hat. They stay on your content and you can still move them, but they stop scoring and are not sold any more.
