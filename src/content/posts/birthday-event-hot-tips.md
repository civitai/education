Civitai's 4th Birthday event runs from November 1 until it ends on **December 1 at 00:00 UTC**, so the last scoring day is November 30. This guide is about that event only, and it stops being useful when the event ends.

You are on one of four color teams and you wear a party hat in your color. A hat sits on one of your own images, models or articles. Whenever other people look at, react to, comment on, sticker or remix the thing wearing your hat, your team scores. Hats keep earning while you are away, so there is nothing you must do every day. The rest of this guide is about getting more out of them.

## The scoreboard {#scoreboard}

These are the points your team earns when someone else acts on an item wearing your hat. The numbers are what the event uses as of November 7, and the event page is the place to check them if they ever change.

| Action by someone else | Points to your team | Where it counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View, signed in, once a day per person | 1 | Images, models, articles |
| Reaction | 5 | Images, articles |
| Comment | 5 | Images, articles |
| Sticker you approve | 10 | Images |
| Remix you accept | 25 | Images |
| Story started from the image | Counts like a remix | Images |
| Thumbs-up review on a model | 5 | Models |

A few rules apply to the whole table:

- Reactions, comments, stickers, remixes and thumbs-up reviews count once per person per item.
- One person can give you at most 50 points a day in total.
- Your own actions on your own hats earn nothing.
- Only actions before the event ends count. After that your hats stay on your work, but they stop scoring.
- A sticker here means a regular sticker from the sticker tray, and it scores once the image's owner approves it. Regular stickers are not affected by Hat Turf.
- Hat Turf is a separate way to score on other people's images, with turf hats you place for free. A worn hat mutes turf: turf hats can still go on an image that wears a hat, but they do not earn until that hat comes off. So the hat you wear on your own work protects what it earns. To learn how turf works, read [Hat Turf: Stick Your Team's Hats on Other People's Images](https://civitai.com/articles/38205/hat-turf-stick-your-teams-hats-on-other-peoples-images).

## Tip 1: Put your hat on your best image {#hat-your-best}

A hat earns only on the exact item it sits on, so pick the thing people will actually open. Hats go on your own images, models and articles. An image has to be in a post before it can wear a hat. Views, reactions and comments on that item are what pile up, so a strong cover or a strong first image is worth the hat.

You can move a hat to another item at any time. There is a 10-minute wait between moves.

## Tip 2: Hat an image people want to remix, then accept fast {#accept-remixes}

An accepted remix is the biggest single payout in the table, worth as much as 25 signed-in views. It only counts after you accept it, so nothing scores while a submission sits in your queue. A pending submission expires after 48 hours, so check your queue every day.

1. Hat an image with a prompt or style that people will want to build on.
2. When a remix arrives, open your user menu, then **Placements**, switch to the **Remixes** tab, and open **Received**. You can go straight there at [civitai.com/user/placements?type=remix&tab=received](https://civitai.com/user/placements?type=remix&tab=received).
3. Accept the ones you like.

## Tip 3: Spend your free remixes on a teammate's hatted image {#free-remixes}

Submitting a remix to someone's gallery usually costs Buzz or your one free placement for the day. During the event there is a better option when you and the image owner are on the same team and the image is wearing a team hat. Those remixes are free, they do not use up your daily free placement, and you get several a day. The submit card tells you how many are left today. The owner still reviews the remix, and the points only count once they accept.

How to do it:

1. Remix a teammate's hatted image with the on-site generator. Free submissions only work for remixes made on Civitai from that image.
2. Post the result. In the post editor, a remix card appears under the image. Submit it to the original image's Remix gallery from there.
3. If the submit card shows a free team remix, submit it. The team waiver is applied for you, so there is nothing to choose. It needs the teammate's gallery to be open, and you both need to have joined the same team.

If you are remixing an image from another team, the ordinary free placement applies instead: one a day, shared between stickers and remix galleries, and one free submission per image. The image owner decides whether to accept.

## Tip 4: Put a hat on your own remix post {#hat-your-remix}

A remix lives in two places. It shows up in the original image's gallery, and it is also your own post. Put a hat on that post's image and it earns views, reactions and comments for you as well. After you submit a remix, open your post and place a hat on the image.

## Tip 5: Reach many different people {#breadth}

One person can give you at most 50 points a day, and most actions count only once. A hundred different people each reacting once beat three fans clicking everything. Post, share the link, and ask real people to take a look.

## Tip 6: Rally your color {#rally}

Teammates are the easiest crowd to reach. Ask them to look, react, comment, add a sticker or try a remix. Start a collection of your team's hatted work, or share it in a group or chat, and say which image is wearing your hat. Ask for real engagement from real people. Never trade reactions.

## Tip 7: Stickers and comments on teammates' hats {#stickers-and-comments}

On a teammate's hatted image, add a reaction and a comment, and place a regular sticker if you have one. Each is a one-time bonus for them per image: 5 for the reaction, 5 for the comment and 10 for an approved sticker. A thoughtful comment helps the creator too, so make it a real one. Read more about stickers in [Civitai's Guide to the Creator Shop and Stickers](/civitais-guide-to-the-creator-shop-and-stickers/).

## Tip 8: Hat a model and collect thumbs-up reviews {#hat-a-model}

Models score views, 1 a day per signed-in person, and thumbs-up reviews, 5 per person. A thumbs-up review counts only while the reviewer still recommends the model. Models tend to keep getting visitors for days, so a hat on a model keeps paying after you log off. Place a hat on a published model and ask people who use it to leave a review.

## Tip 9: Move your hat to what is working {#follow-the-views}

Hats keep earning while you are away. If one of your items is taking off, you can move a hat onto it. Check which of your items are getting views, and re-place your hat there. There is a 10-minute wait between moves, so do not bounce it around.

## Play it straight {#fair-play}

Fine: asking real people to look, react, comment, sticker and remix, and sharing your team's collections and posts. Not fine: extra accounts, bought or bot engagement, reaction rings of throwaway accounts, and mass low-effort reposts made only to carry hats. Those can be removed from the event.
