## One currency, three colors

![Three Buzz icons: Blue for free rewards and safe-for-work use, Green for civitai.com with PG and PG-13 content, Yellow for civitai.red with all ratings](/images/guides/buzz-colors/buzz-colors-cover.webp)

**Buzz** is Civitai's on-site currency. You spend it on image and video generation, on training, on early access to models, on tips, and on profile cosmetics. Your balance is split into three colors, and each color has its own rules for how you get it and where you can spend it.

| Color | Where it lives | How you get it | Can be bought | Content it covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Blue** | civitai.com and civitai.red | Free rewards and bonuses | No | Safe for work |
| **Green** | civitai.com | Purchase, membership, tips, creator earnings | Yes | PG and PG-13 |
| **Yellow** | civitai.red | Purchase, tips, creator earnings | Yes | All ratings, including mature |

Civitai runs two sites. **civitai.com** is the safe-for-work site: it shows PG and PG-13 content and uses Green Buzz. **civitai.red** is where unrestricted content creation now lives: it uses Yellow Buzz. Your account and your Blue Buzz work on both.

:::note
Older posts and some third-party pages still mention civitai.green. That address now goes to civitai.com, and Green Buzz is spent there.
:::

## Blue Buzz

Blue Buzz is the free color. It is meant to let anyone create without paying.

**How you get it**

- New accounts are granted Blue Buzz during onboarding, as a gift for on-site generation (100 Buzz at the time of writing).
- Daily and community rewards. The list changes over time, and the current one is on your [Buzz Dashboard](https://civitai.com/user/buzz-dashboard) under Rewards. Examples are reacting to content, making your first post of the day, following new creators, giving feedback on generator results, and getting reactions on your own work.
- Daily rewards reset at midnight UTC.
- Memberships multiply Blue Buzz rewards. The membership page lists 1.5x for Bronze, 2.5x for Silver and 4x for Gold on daily rewards.
- Bonus Blue Buzz on large purchases. A Buzz purchase of 250,000 Buzz or more adds bonus Blue Buzz, and the bonus grows at higher amounts. It does not stack with the membership purchase bonus: the larger of the two applies.
- Referrals. A new member who joins with a referral code gets a one-time Blue Buzz bonus, equal to 25% of the tier's monthly Buzz, on their first paid membership month. The person who shared the code earns membership-perk Tokens for up to three paid months per friend. Once that friend has a paid membership, the sharer also gets 10% back in Blue Buzz on the Buzz the friend buys.

**What it buys**

- Image and video generation.
- LoRA training.
- Early access to a model, when the creator accepts Blue Buzz.
- Shop items that accept Blue Buzz. The shop has an "Accepts Blue Buzz" filter.

**What it can't do**

- It is safe-for-work only. On civitai.red, a generation paid with Blue Buzz is blocked if it produces mature results, unless you are a paid member.
- It can't be tipped to other users, and it doesn't count toward Creator Program earnings. It is not "bankable".
- It can't be purchased.

## Green Buzz

Green Buzz is the paid color for civitai.com. It covers PG and PG-13 content.

**How you get it**

- **Membership.** Each paid tier delivers Green Buzz every month: Bronze ($10) gives 10,000, Silver ($25) gives 25,000 and Gold ($50) gives 50,000. Memberships also add a bonus on Buzz you buy (5%, 10% and 20%). See the [membership page](https://civitai.com/pricing).
- **Buying Buzz** at [civitai.com/purchase/buzz](https://civitai.com/purchase/buzz), by card. You can also reach it from your Buzz balance in the site header. The page asks you to confirm that Green Buzz can't generate mature content and that the purchase is non-refundable. Your membership's purchase bonus (5%, 10% or 20%) applies.
- **Tips** from other creators. The [Buzz Beggars Board](https://civitai.com/collections/3870938) is a collection where people post images hoping to be tipped.
- **Creator earnings** when others use your resources, if you are in the Creator Program. The program needs an active Bronze, Silver or Gold membership and a Creator Score of 40,000 or more.

**What it buys**

- Image and video generation and LoRA training.
- Early access to models.
- Tips to creators.
- Profile badges and cosmetics from the shop.

## Yellow Buzz

Yellow Buzz is the paid color for civitai.red, where all content ratings are allowed.

**How you get it**

- **Buying Buzz** on civitai.red. The Buy Buzz page there states that purchased Buzz supports all content ratings. Crypto is the payment method: see [Purchasing Buzz with Crypto](/civitais-guide-to-purchasing-buzz-with-crypto/).
- **Tips** from other creators.
- **Creator earnings** from the Creator Program, under the same requirements as on Green.
- **Bounties.** Bounties are offered on civitai.red, and completing one earns Buzz. They are funded with Yellow Buzz there.

**What it buys**

- Image and video generation, including mature content, and LoRA training.
- Early access to models.
- Tips and bounties.
- Profile cosmetics.

**Good to know**

- Yellow Buzz can't be spent on civitai.com. If you select Yellow while generating there, the generator tells you to switch to Green or go to civitai.red.
- Yellow memberships are no longer sold. A civitai.com membership carries over to civitai.red, and it delivers Green Buzz.
- Yellow Buzz is bankable, like Green: it counts toward Creator Program earnings. Blue does not.

## Which Buzz is spent first

The site you are on decides which paid color applies: Green on civitai.com, Yellow on civitai.red. Blue is the only other color you can pay with on either site.

- **In the generator,** you choose. The cost button next to Generate opens a "Pay with" menu that lists Blue and your site's color, each with its balance. It starts on your site's color until you pick another one, and the generator remembers your pick. On civitai.com the menu also lists Yellow if you have a Yellow balance. Picking it shows a notice that Yellow can't be used on civitai.com, with buttons to switch to Green or go to civitai.red. One generation is paid from one color. If the selected balance is too low, the generator offers a button such as "Switch to Green Buzz" for a color you have enough of.
- **In LoRA training,** you choose the same way.
- **In the shop,** items whose creators accept Blue Buzz give you a choice, including a "Blue + Green" option ("Use your Blue first, the rest in Green") on civitai.com and the Yellow equivalent on civitai.red.
- **For tips, bounties, auctions and membership purchases,** you pay with your site's color only.
- **For paid early access,** you choose Blue or your site's color when the creator accepts Blue.

If you want to keep your paid Buzz for tips and early access, pay for generations with Blue.

## Green Buzz and PG-13 content

civitai.com shows PG and PG-13 content only. Green Buzz follows the same line: it covers safe-for-work generation and can't be used to generate content rated R, X or XXX. For mature work, use civitai.red with Yellow Buzz. The Green purchase page asks you to acknowledge this, and Green purchases are non-refundable, so decide which site you need before you buy.

## Worked example

You are on civitai.com with 300 Blue Buzz and 1,000 Green Buzz. A generation costs 120 Buzz.

1. Open the "Pay with" menu next to Generate. It lists "Green Buzz" and "Blue Buzz" with those balances, and it starts on Green.
2. Choose Blue. The generator charges 120 from Blue, leaving 180 Blue and 1,000 Green.
3. Later, a tip of 200 Buzz can only come from Green, because tips never use Blue. Green drops to 800.
4. When the Blue balance falls below the cost of a generation, the generator offers "Switch to Green Buzz".

Spending Blue first on generation keeps your paid Buzz free for tips and early access.

## Where to buy Buzz now

- **Crypto.** Yellow Buzz on civitai.red can be bought by sending crypto to your personal deposit address. The steps are in [Purchasing Buzz with Crypto](/civitais-guide-to-purchasing-buzz-with-crypto/). The Coinbase Commerce checkout was removed, but sending from Coinbase.com or any other wallet to your deposit address still works.
- **Card, for Green Buzz.** On civitai.com, Buy Buzz takes a card payment and credits Green Buzz. Buy Buzz on civitai.red is crypto only.
- **Membership.** A civitai.com membership is a subscription that delivers Green Buzz each month. You can still gift a membership from the membership page.
- **Gift codes** for Buzz can no longer be bought.
- **Perks membership.** civitai.red also offers one month of membership perks for earned Buzz. It has no monthly Buzz, no bonus Buzz and no Creator Program, and it doesn't renew on its own.

Prices and packages change, so check the Buy Buzz page for the current options.

## Checking your balance

Sign in and open your [Buzz Dashboard](https://civitai.com/user/buzz-dashboard). It shows each color on its own, your recent transactions for each, and the current Blue Buzz rewards, with a progress bar for each daily reward. Your balance is also in the site header. In the generator, the "Pay with" menu shows the balance of each color you can spend.

## Troubleshooting

- **A generation on civitai.red was blocked when paid with Blue Buzz.** Blue Buzz is safe for work only. A result rated mature is blocked. Pay with Yellow Buzz instead, or become a member.
- **The generator says Yellow Buzz can't be used on civitai.com.** Switch to Green Buzz, or go to civitai.red where Yellow is spent.
- **You can't tip with Blue Buzz.** Tips use your site's color only. Use Green on civitai.com or Yellow on civitai.red.
- **A reward is missing.** Daily rewards have a cap and reset at midnight UTC. The Rewards section of the Buzz Dashboard shows what you have earned today.
