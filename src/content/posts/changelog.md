We’re excited to announce the launch of our new Changelog, where we’ll keep you informed about all the major additions, fixes, and changes made to [Civitai.com](https://www.civitai.com)!

![Civitai Changelog Graphic](http://education.civitai.com//wp-content/uploads/2024/08/changelog_image-1-1024x701.png)

While we won’t log every minor tweak, you can expect updates on any changes that have a visible impact on your experience. We’ll keep the notes brief and to the point, with a monthly recap to ensure you’re always in the loop.

Stay tuned for updates as we continue to improve your experience on Civitai!
