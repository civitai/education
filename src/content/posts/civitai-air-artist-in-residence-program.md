We’re thrilled to announce the launch of [Civitai AiR](https://air.civitai.com/), our groundbreaking Artist-in-Residence Program designed to redefine the boundaries of creativity and technology. Civitai AiR serves as a digital incubator and studio, offering a unique platform for artists to explore, innovate, and create with generative AI.

What’s in store?

-   A $1000 grant to fuel your creative journey
-   Unlimited access to Civitai’s generative AI resources
-   Collaboration & Support from top community AI practitioners
-   Online exhibition and living archive of your work
-   Visibility and promotion to our community of millions

Join us in this exciting journey to explore the untapped potential of AI in art. Whether you’re a traditional fine artist curious about AI or a technologist and AI native, Civitai AiR is your studio to push the boundaries of contemporary art today.

Apply now to be part of an innovative program that promises not just to envision the future of art but to create it. Let’s embark on this creative adventure together!
