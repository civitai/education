## What Challenges are

![Challenges in three steps: Create, Submit, Win](/images/guides/challenges/challenges-cover.webp)

A **Challenge** is a themed art contest. Civitai gives you a theme and, often, a model to use. You make images, submit your best ones before the deadline, and an AI judge scores them. The top entries win Buzz.

The current list is always at [civitai.com/challenges](https://civitai.com/challenges). You can browse it without signing in, and you need an account to enter.

There are three kinds:

| Kind | Who runs it | Entry fee | Prizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Challenges** | Civitai | None | Set by Civitai for each challenge |
| **Featured events** | Civitai | None | Set by Civitai for each event |
| **Community Challenges** | Members (and some from Civitai staff) | Set by the creator | The creator's seed plus the entry fees |

:::note
Contest announcements on this site are news posts. They are not how-tos and they go out of date. The challenges page is where the live rules, prizes and deadlines are.
:::

## Find a Challenge {#find-a-challenge}

Open [civitai.com/challenges](https://civitai.com/challenges). From top to bottom the page shows:

- **Featured events**, when one is running.
- **Your Challenges**, a row that shows once you have entered or created a challenge. Choose **See all** for the full list, which has three filters: **Participated**, **Tracking** and **Created**.
- **Daily Challenges**. Each day's challenge features a specific AI model. The small info icon next to the title opens "How Challenges Work". **Previous winners** links to the [winners page](https://civitai.com/challenges/winners).
- **Community Challenges**, with sort and status filters and a **Create Challenge** button.

Open a challenge to see its page. The **Overview** section lists when it starts and ends, the maximum entries per person, how entries are reviewed and the allowed content ratings. The prizes are in a separate **Prizes** section and, when a challenge lists them, the scoring categories in **Judging**. Always read them. Rules differ from one challenge to the next.

You can also turn on notifications with the bell icon in a challenge's header when you are signed in. Its label reads "Notify me when this starts" before it begins and "Notify me before this ends" while it runs.

## Enter a Challenge {#enter-a-challenge}

1. Open the challenge. If it requires a model, press **Generate**. The generator opens with the challenge's model already selected. Make images that follow the theme.
2. Press **Submit** (or **Submit Entries**).
3. In the **Submit Challenge Entry** dialog, choose images from one of three tabs: **My Images**, **From Generator** or **Upload New**. The dialog title shows how many entries you have used ("Your Entries: 2 / 5") and the allowed ratings.
4. If a **Category** list appears, pick one for each submission.
5. If the challenge has an entry fee, the **Submit** button shows the total Buzz and the footer breaks it down. Choosing it pays and submits your entries.

Your entry has to meet the rules of the challenge:

- **Created after the challenge started.** An image made before the start is rejected ("Created before challenge").
- **Allowed content rating.** An image outside the challenge's allowed ratings is rejected ("NSFW restricted").
- **Required model.** If the challenge lists eligible models, the image's generation data must show at least one of them. Two messages can appear: "Wrong model" means the image used a different model. "Model not detected" means the image carries no generation data that names the model, which is typical of images made off-site. Generate on Civitai with the featured model to be safe.
- **Within your entry limit.** The limit is shown on the challenge page and in the dialog.
- **Not your own challenge.** You can't submit to a challenge you created.

If you recently won a challenge of the same kind, a blue banner says you are on a **winner cooldown** until the date shown. A daily win affects daily challenges, and an event tracks its own wins. You can still submit, but your entries are skipped when the top three are picked. The cooldown is usually 7 days, and if every entrant is on cooldown, entries are picked from everyone anyway. It does not apply to challenges created by members.

## How judging works {#how-judging-works}

Entries are reviewed by an AI judge, not by votes.

- **Daily Challenges.** The top 3 entries are chosen by the AI judging system and ranked by a weighted score. Theme relevance counts for 50%, so staying on the theme matters most. Daily Challenges have no entry fee, so not every entry is reviewed: the Overview section says "Only 6–12 entries selected at random every 10 min". Submit your best work rather than your most images.
- **Challenges with an entry fee.** The Overview section says "Every entry is judged".
- **Paid review.** Some challenges let you pay Buzz to guarantee your entries get judged. If yours does, the submit dialog shows a "Guarantee entries get judged" checkbox, and your entries panel shows the price per entry.
- **Judge and categories.** Each challenge has its own judge. Community Challenges list their scoring categories and weights on the challenge page.

Completed challenges show the judge's commentary and final verdict on the challenge page.

## Prizes {#prizes}

- **Placement prizes.** The top three entries win Buzz, and the **Prizes** section on each challenge shows the amount for each place. Winners also earn challenge points.
- **Participation prize.** Some Civitai challenges give a small Buzz prize to everyone who submits enough valid entries. The Overview section shows the requirement, for example "Min 10 entries to qualify". Participation prizes are paid directly in Blue Buzz. Challenges created by members do not have one.
- **Claiming a placement prize.** Placement prizes are not paid straight into your balance. When you win, you get a notification and a **Claim prize** button on [civitai.com/prizes](https://civitai.com/prizes). On civitai.com you receive Green Buzz. On civitai.red you choose Green or Yellow. A prize you don't claim is paid automatically as Green Buzz after 30 days. See [Buzz Colors: Blue, Yellow and Green](/buzz-colors-blue-yellow-and-green/). Prizes from Community Challenges are claimed the same way, so you receive the color you pick when claiming, not necessarily the color of the challenge.

Check each challenge's own page for its prize amounts, since they can change.

## Community Challenges {#community-challenges}

Any member who meets the requirements can run a challenge. The creator picks the theme, rules, schedule and judge. Entrants pay an entry fee, and the fees fund the prize. The fee, refund and prize-pool rules below apply to member-created challenges. A staff-created challenge in the same list follows the Overview and Prizes on its own page.

### Entering a Community Challenge

It works like any other challenge, with an entry fee and a few differences:

- The fee is charged on each submitted entry. Of each fee, 25 Buzz covers the AI judging and platform costs. The rest goes into the prize pool.
- The 25 Buzz judging cut is never refunded. If the challenge is canceled, or finishes with no judged entries, the prize-pool portion of your fee is returned, and the creator gets the initial prize back.
- You can only enter once the challenge is Active, and the Submit button does not show before then. A challenge becomes Active on the hour after its start time, so Submit can appear up to an hour late. If you try too early, you may see "Challenge is starting shortly, please try again in a few minutes".
- Every entry is judged.
- The fee is charged in the challenge's Buzz color, Green or Yellow. Green challenges are safe for work and show on civitai.com. Yellow challenges show on civitai.red.

The prize pool is the creator's initial prize plus the entry fee minus 25 for each paid entry. The challenge page shows the projected split of the current pool while it runs.

### Who can create one {#who-can-create}

- A creator score of at least 5,000.
- An account in good standing: not banned, not muted, and without active strikes serious enough to mute you.
- No more than 5 Community Challenges created in any 24 hours.
- A limit on challenges that are Scheduled or Active at once, by membership: Free 1, Bronze and Founder 2, Silver 3, Gold 5. See [Civitai's Guide to Membership Tiers](/civitais-guide-to-membership-tiers/).

Open **Create Challenge** on the challenges page, or go to [civitai.com/challenges/create](https://civitai.com/challenges/create). If you do not meet a requirement, the page lists it.

### The form

**Basic information**

- **Title** (3 to 200 characters).
- **Theme**: a short theme, 1 or 2 words works well (up to 100 characters), such as "Neon Dreams".
- **Theme Elements**: visual cues the judge uses when scoring, separated by commas. Leave it empty to generate them from the theme.
- **Cover Image** (required): 1024 by 768 (4:3) is suggested.
- **Description** (required, up to 5,000 characters): explain the challenge and its rules.
- **Eligible Models** (optional, up to 20): entries must use at least one of them. Leave empty to allow any model.

**Schedule**

- It must start at least 3 hours from now and at most 30 days from now.
- It must run between 24 hours and 30 days.
- It shows in the feed from 7 days before it starts. It is scanned before it becomes visible, and you cannot receive entries until the scan passes and the challenge goes Active.

**Entry fee and prizes**

- **Entry Fee**: from 50 to 100,000 Buzz. Of each fee, 25 Buzz is not added to the prize pool.
- **Initial Prize** (optional): Buzz you seed the pool with, up to 10,000,000. It is charged to you when you create the challenge.
- **Prize split**: the percentages for 1st, 2nd and 3rd place. They must total 100% (the default is 50, 30, 20).
- **Buzz color** (a Yellow Buzz / Green Buzz toggle under Entry Fee & Prizes): choose Green or Yellow. The form starts on your site's color. Green challenges are safe for work only and show on civitai.com. Yellow challenges show on civitai.red. Entry fees, the initial prize and the pool all use that color, and the color can't change once an initial prize has been charged.

**Entry requirements**

- **Max Entries Per User**: 1 to 100 (the form starts at 5).
- **Allowed ratings**: the content ratings entries may have, from PG up to XXX, with presets "SFW Only", "Include Mature" and "All Content". It starts on safe for work. Green challenges are limited to PG and PG-13.
- **Max Participants** (optional): once reached, no new participants can join.

**Judging**

- **Assigned Judge**: choose an AI judge persona from the list.
- **Categories**: scoring categories with weights that must total 100%. You choose up to 4. Theme is required, and you can add up to three more from the category library (for example Creativity, Aesthetic, Technical Quality, Humor or Cinematics). The form starts with Theme 50, Wittiness 15, Humor 15 and Aesthetic 20.

After you submit, a moderation scan runs and your challenge goes live when it passes. A challenge that is blocked, or whose scan is still unfinished a day after its start, is canceled and the fees and initial prize are refunded. You can follow it under **Your Challenges** > **Created**. You can edit a challenge only while it is Scheduled and has no entries, using **Edit Challenge** in the menu on the challenge page. Once it is Active it is locked. You can also delete it while it is Scheduled with no entries, which refunds your initial prize. The initial prize can't be changed by editing.

## Worked example

You create a Community Challenge on civitai.com called "Neon Dreams".

- Entry fee 200 Buzz, initial prize 1,000 Buzz, prize split 60 / 25 / 15.
- Of each 200 Buzz fee, 25 pays for judging and 175 goes to the pool.

Twenty entries are submitted. The prize pool is 1,000 + (20 x 175) = 4,500 Buzz.

| Place | Share | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 60% | 2,700 Buzz |
| 2nd | 25% | 1,125 Buzz |
| 3rd | 15% | 675 Buzz |

Separately, suppose you enter someone else's challenge with the same numbers. You can't enter your own. You submit three entries at 200 Buzz each, 600 Buzz in all. 525 Buzz of that goes to the pool and 75 Buzz pays for judging. If your best entry places in the top three, you win that place's prize. A person can win only one place, even with several entries.

## Troubleshooting

- **Submit is missing or disabled.** The challenge may not be Active yet (Community Challenges become Active on the hour after they start), it may have ended, your account may be muted, or it is your own challenge. Check the Starts and Ends rows in the Overview section.
- **"Created before challenge".** The image was made before the challenge started. Make a new one.
- **"Wrong model" or "Model not detected".** Generate with the challenge's model on Civitai using the **Generate** button on the challenge page.
- **"NSFW restricted".** The image's rating is outside what the challenge allows. See the allowed ratings in the Overview section.
- **My entry was not reviewed on a Daily Challenge.** Daily Challenges review a random selection. Challenges with an entry fee review every entry.
- **My entries won't place.** If you won a recent challenge of the same kind you are on a winner cooldown. The blue banner in the Submit dialog shows when it ends.
- **I won but have no Buzz.** Placement prizes must be claimed at [civitai.com/prizes](https://civitai.com/prizes). Unclaimed prizes are paid as Green Buzz after 30 days.
- **I can't create a challenge.** The create page lists the requirements you do not meet yet: creator score, account standing, the daily limit or your membership's limit on running challenges.
- **Challenge starting shortly.** A Community Challenge accepts entries only once it is Active. Try again in a few minutes. If your account has only Blue Buzz, you can't pay an entry fee: fees are charged in the challenge's color, Green or Yellow.
