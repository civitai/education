## The Civitai Creators Program {#what-are-clubs}

**2025 Update**! We’re evolving the Creator Program – for more information, [check out our announcement!](https://civitai.com/articles/11163) The Current Creator Program Beta Test has been wound-down, and the new Program will shortly be opened up to all Civitai users!

What was formerly **`Creator Clubs`** has evolved, via excellent community feedback, into the [Civitai Creators Program](https://civitai.com/creators-program).

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Screenshot-2024-01-25-085811.webp)](https://civitai.com/creators-program)

## How do I join the Creators Program

Creators interested in joining the program must meet the following requirements:

-   Reside within a [supported country](https://stripe.com/docs/connect/cross-border-payouts#supported-countries)

-   Must maintain an account is in good standing

-   Be 18 or older

You must also meet a minimum follower requirement of **1000 followers.** Applicants will be picked based upon

-   Unique Downloads

-   Unique Generations

-   Unique Reaction To Your Content

Unique Engagement (comments/reviews)

## FAQ

**Why a 30% platform fee on withdrawals?**

While some of that fee goes to covering payment processing and currency conversion, most of it goes towards offsetting the cost of the Buzz we give out to users for the rewards system. That’s the Buzz you get for reacting to content, posting images, receiving reactions and more. Its important to keep this Buzz in circulation, since it encourages users to engage with your resources and content. Since introducing the Buzz reward system we’ve seen a 60% increase in the number of people that react to content each day.

**I make NSFW/Celebrity/Anime/Furry resources and content, can I still join?**

Yes, you’re not restricted from the program based on your content.

**How and when will I get paid?**

You can withdraw a minimum of 100,000 ?and maximum of 100,000,000 ? each day. Direct $ Tips and donations do not count toward these limits.

**Can I withdraw my earnings in currencies other than USD?**

Yes, you can withdraw in your native currency.

**Can I leave the Creator Program?**

Anytime you want. It’s a voluntary program.

**How will changes to the program be communicated?**

Changes will be communicated via email, onsite notification and/or direct message.

## I need more help!
