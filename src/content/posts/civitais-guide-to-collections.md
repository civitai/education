A **collection** is a list you build out of things on Civitai: models, images, posts, articles or 3D models. You can keep one to yourself, share it with a link, or make it public so other people can follow it as you add to it. Some collections are open, and anyone can submit to them.

This guide covers [saving to a collection](#saving), [creating one](#creating), [who can see it](#privacy), [following](#following), [contributing](#contributing), and [running a shared collection](#collaborators) with collaborators and a review queue. If you are looking for feeds built from sources instead, that is [Hubs](/civitais-guide-to-hubs/). See [Collections and hubs](#hubs) for how the two differ.

## What goes in a collection {#types}

Every collection holds **one type** of thing. You pick the type when you create it, and you cannot change it afterwards.

| Type | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Model | Models |
| Image | Images |
| Post | Posts |
| Article | Articles |
| 3D Model | 3D models |

When you save something, the picker only shows collections of the right type. A model goes into a Model collection, an image into an Image collection, and so on.

## Finding collections {#finding}

You need to be signed in to save, create or follow. Open [civitai.com/collections](https://civitai.com/collections). If you own a collection, it opens your first one. Signed out, or if you own no collections, you see a short "What are Collections?" card.

When you are signed in, the **My Collections** panel on the left lists the collections you own, follow or contribute to. You can search it, filter by type ("All types"), and filter by your role ("Any role"). Each person's profile also has a **Collections** tab with their public collections.

A collection page shows its cover, name, description, owner and items. Public collections can be opened signed out.

## Saving to a collection {#saving}

1. Find something you want to keep. On a model, image, post, article or 3D model, use **Save**. On a model page, click the bookmark icon (tooltip "Add to collection"), or open Share and choose Save. On images and posts in a feed, the menu item reads "Save image to collection" or "Save post to collection". Everywhere else it reads Save.
2. A window titled "Add to Model Collection" (or Image, Post, Article, 3D Model) opens. Collections are grouped as **Your collections**, **Shared with you** and **Collections you follow**. Use the search box to find one.
3. Tick a collection to add the item. Untick one that is marked as saved to remove the item. Nothing changes until you click **Save** at the bottom of the window.
4. To make a new one, click **New collection** in the same window. Searching for a name that does not exist offers to create it. This quick form takes the type from the item and adds the item to the new collection. It asks for a name and description, privacy, who can add (members only) and the mature content checkbox, and has no cover image or type picker. Click **Create**.

Where an item shows up on a collection you don't own depends on how that collection takes entries. See [Contributing to someone else's collection](#contributing).

## Creating a collection {#creating}

1. Open [civitai.com/collections](https://civitai.com/collections) and click **Create** next to **My Collections**. On a narrow screen, or with the sidebar collapsed, there is no Create button: use **New collection** in the Save window instead (see [Saving to a collection](#saving), step 4), or open the page on a wider screen.
2. In the **Create collection** window, fill in:
   - **Cover Image**: shown on the collection card and page. The cover you pick in this window is not kept when the collection is created, so set it afterwards: open the collection's menu, choose **Edit collection** and add it there.
   - **Name**: required, up to 30 characters.
   - **Description**: what the collection is for, up to 300 characters.
   - **Privacy**: see [Who can see a collection](#privacy).
   - **Collection Type**: Model, Image, Post, Article or 3D Model. This is fixed once the collection exists.
   - **Who can add to this collection**: see [Opening a collection to others](#opening). If you leave it alone, it is **Me and my collaborators**.
   - Under Extras, a **This collection contains mature content** checkbox.
3. Save it. The collection appears in My Collections. If you leave Privacy and Who can add alone, the collection is Private and closed to others.

To change a collection later, open its menu and choose **Edit collection**. Only the owner can change privacy and who can add. A Manager can change the name, description and cover.

## Who can see a collection {#privacy}

| Privacy | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Only you and the collaborators you invite, unless the collection is also open to submissions. Then anyone with the link can open the page, follow it and submit, but they can't see the items in it. |
| **Public** | Anyone. |
| **Unlisted** | Only people who have the link. |

To share a collection, set it to **Public** or **Unlisted** and send the address of the collection page. Unlisted is for sharing with chosen people. Anyone who has the link can open it.

A private collection can still be shared with named people by [inviting them as collaborators](#collaborators).

## Following a collection {#following}

Follow a collection to keep it close. Open it and click **Follow**. A followed Public or Unlisted collection appears in My Collections. If the collection is open to submissions, it also appears under **Collections you follow** in the Save window. A Private collection that is open to submissions appears only in the Save window. Followers get a notification when new items are added. Click **Unfollow** to stop. You can't follow your own collection.

If you are a collaborator, the button does not apply to you. To step down from a shared collection, open its menu and choose **Leave collection**, then confirm. You lose the collaborator role and stop following it. You get the role back only if someone invites you again.

Nobody sees a list of who follows a collection.

## Contributing to someone else's collection {#contributing}

How you can add to a collection is set by its owner. The options are:

| Setting | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Me and my collaborators** | Only the owner and invited collaborators can add. |
| **Anyone, no review** | You can add, and your entry appears right away. |
| **Anyone, with review** | You can submit. The owner or a Manager approves your entry before it appears. |

Image and Post collections that are open to submissions also show a **Submit an entry** button on the collection page, for anyone who can add and isn't the owner. On an Image collection it opens a window titled "Add images to collection", with a **Create a new image post** button and your own images to pick from, then **Add**. On a Post collection it takes you to the post creator. Model, Article and 3D Model collections are added to through **Save**. To add one of those to someone else's open collection, open the collection and click **Follow** first. It then shows in the Save window under **Collections you follow**. Owners add to their own Image collection with the plus button ("Add from your library") or **Save**.

You can still pick **Anyone, with review** on a 3D Model collection, but saving a 3D model to it as anyone other than the owner or a collaborator fails with "3D models cannot be submitted to collections that review entries". Use **Anyone, no review**, or keep it to collaborators.

Some points to know:

- **Adding to a collection you submitted to makes you follow it.** That is how it stays in your save picker.
- **Some collections have extra rules.** Collections run by Civitai staff can cap how many entries one person has, ask for a tag or a title, or hold entries for a period. Owners of ordinary collections can't set these. For example, the staff-run [Beggars Board](https://civitai.com/collections/3870938) says entries are reviewed before they show, you can have 2 images visible at once, and approved images are displayed for 3 days. The page of each collection says what applies.
- **Removing your entry.** On Model and Image collections, open the item's menu on the collection page and choose **Remove from this Collection**. That also works for the item's author. On any collection, the person who added an item, the owner and Managers can untick it in the Save window and click **Save**. Post, Article and 3D Model collections have no menu item, so use Save.

Contests are special collections that Civitai staff run. They have their own entry rules and are not covered here.

## Opening a collection to others {#opening}

You can let other people add to a collection you own. Opening it to submissions is a **member feature**, at any membership tier. Without a membership, the **Who can add to this collection** setting is shown but disabled, with a **Get a membership** button. See the [membership page](https://civitai.com/pricing).

Open the collection's menu, choose **Edit collection**, and set **Who can add to this collection** to one of:

- **Me and my collaborators**: closed to the public.
- **Anyone, no review**: entries show immediately. Choose this for friendly, low-risk collections.
- **Anyone, with review**: entries wait for approval. Choose this for anything public.

Set **Privacy** to Public or Unlisted when you open a collection to submissions. A Private collection that allows submissions can still be opened, followed and submitted to by anyone who has its link.

If the owner's membership ends, the public can no longer submit and new collaborators can't be invited. Existing items stay. The owner keeps full access, and Managers keep their review and edit rights. On a collection set to **Anyone, no review**, invited Contributors and Managers also stop being able to add until the owner renews. Managers can still review and edit. On **Anyone, with review** or **Me and my collaborators**, collaborators keep adding. The change is applied by a daily check, so it is not instant. The banner the owner sees says entries are paused. That applies to other people, and the owner can still add. Renewing reopens the collection.

### Reviewing entries {#review}

For a collection set to **Anyone, with review**, open the menu and choose **Review items**. Entries waiting for a decision are listed there. The page has a **Sort by** menu (Newest or Oldest). Select entries (the toolbar has **Select all**, **Clear selection**, **Accept**, **Reject** and **Refresh** icon buttons, with tooltips), then Accept, which asks you to confirm, or Reject. Accepting adds the entry to the collection. Rejecting opens a small window where you can pick an optional reason from a list, which the submitter sees in their notification. Either way, the entry leaves the queue. Collections of 3D models can't use this queue. The collection page also shows a **Review** button when entries are waiting.

The owner, Managers and invited collaborators post straight through without waiting in the queue.

## Collaborators and roles {#collaborators}

Collaborators are named people you invite to help run a collection. They are different from followers. Open the collection's menu and choose **Collaborators**.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Contributor** | Add items without going through the review queue, and remove items they added. |
| **Manager** | Everything a Contributor can do, plus remove anyone's item, review entries, edit the name, description and cover, and invite Contributors. |

The owner is always in charge. Only the owner can make someone a Manager, change a Manager's role, remove a Manager, or change privacy and who can add.

To invite someone:

1. In **Collaborators**, type their name in **Invite by username** and pick them from the list.
2. Choose a role in the dropdown next to the name field, then click **Invite**.
3. They see it under **Invitations** in their My Collections panel, in a notification, and as a banner on the collection if they open it. They can **Accept** or **Decline**.
4. Pending invites show in the **Pending invites** list, where you can **Resend** or **Cancel** one. Resending starts the 7 days again.

Things to know:

- Invites expire after 7 days. Send a new one if an invite lapses.
- A collection can have up to 25 collaborators, and no more than 5 of them can be Managers. Pending invites count toward both limits.
- Inviting collaborators is a member feature, like opening a collection to submissions.
- Followers are never listed as collaborators.
- To remove a collaborator, the owner or a Manager clicks the trash icon on their row. The owner also has a role dropdown on each row. Managers can remove Contributors only.

## Collections and hubs {#hubs}

A **hub** is a feed of images built from model, creator and tag sources. A collection is a list you curate by hand. They are different tools, and **collections can't be a hub source yet**. If you want a feed that follows a creator or a model, use [Civitai's Guide to Hubs](/civitais-guide-to-hubs/).

## Deleting or reporting {#deleting}

- **Delete collection** is in the menu of a collection you own. A confirmation says deletion is destructive and cannot be reversed.
- **Report collection** is in the menu of any collection you don't own.

## A worked example {#worked-example}

You want a public gallery of neon city images that other people can add to. Steps 3, 5 and 7 need a membership, because they open the collection to other people. Without one, do steps 1, 2 and 4, leave the setting on **Me and my collaborators**, and share the link from step 6 so people can follow it. Only you can add.

1. On the Collections page, click **Create**.
2. Set the Name to "Neon Cityscapes", add a Description, set **Privacy** to **Public**, and **Collection Type** to **Image**.
3. Under **Who can add to this collection**, choose **Anyone, with review**. This needs a membership.
4. Save. Open the collection's menu, choose **Edit collection** and add a Cover Image. Then open an image you like, choose **Save**, tick "Neon Cityscapes" and click **Save** at the bottom of the window.
5. Open the collection's menu, choose **Collaborators**, type a friend's username, pick them, set the role to **Manager** and click **Invite**. They accept from their Invitations list or the banner on the collection.
6. Share the address of the collection page. Other people open it and, if you chose to open it to submissions, submit images with **Submit an entry**. You add your own with the plus button or **Save**.
7. Entries arrive in **Review items**. You or your Manager select the good ones and click **Accept**, then confirm.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| The collection you want isn't in the Save window | A collection only takes its own type, and only if you may add to it. Check the type. For someone else's open collection, open it and click **Follow** first, or ask to be invited as a collaborator. |
| **Who can add to this collection** is disabled | Opening a collection to submissions needs a membership. |
| "This collection is not accepting new collaborators right now." | The owner has no active membership. Collaborators can be invited again once the owner renews. |
| A collection is greyed out in the Save window with "This collection isn't accepting new entries right now." | The owner's membership ended. See [Opening a collection to others](#opening). |
| Your collection "has stopped accepting entries" | The owner's membership ended, so the public can't submit. The owner keeps full access. Renew to reopen it. |
| "This invite has expired." | Invites last 7 days. Ask the owner to send a new one. |
| "You have reached the maximum number of items in collection" | A staff-run collection limits entries per person. Remove one of yours, or wait until one is rejected or expires. |
| You can't change the collection type | The type is fixed when you create the collection. Make a new one of the right type. |
| Your entry isn't showing | On a review collection, other people don't see your entry until it is approved, but you should see it yourself. If you can't see it either, it may have been rejected, or the collection may be Private (see the next row). Otherwise ask the owner. |
| You don't see a **Follow** button | You own the collection, or you are a collaborator on it, or it is a Private collection that is closed to submissions. |
| I submitted to a Private collection and can't see it | Private collections show no items to visitors, including your own entry. Ask the owner. |
| A friend can't open your link | The collection is Private. Set it to Unlisted or Public, or invite them as a collaborator. |
