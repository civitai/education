## What Comics are

![Comics in three steps: Read, Create, Publish](/images/guides/comics/comics-cover.webp)

**Comics** on Civitai are stories told in panels, made with AI image generation. Anyone can read them. Members can also make their own with the **Comic Creator**, which keeps your panels, characters and chapters in one project and publishes them chapter by chapter.

This guide has two halves:

- [Read comics](#read-comics): find a comic, read it, follow it and support its creator.
- [Make a comic](#make-a-comic): start a project, build panels and publish chapters.

:::note
The Comic Creator is a Beta feature. Civitai says it has a limited feature set, that you may run into bumps, and that refunds are not issued for issues related to the Beta. Check the cost shown on each Generate button before you confirm it.
:::

## Find a comic {#find-a-comic}

- **The Comics page.** Choose **Comics** in the site's top navigation (that is its default place, and it can be moved), or go to [civitai.com/comics](https://civitai.com/comics). A row of genres sits at the top. Pick one, or choose All to see everything.
- **Search.** Use the site search and switch to Comics, or go to [civitai.com/search/comics](https://civitai.com/search/comics).
- **A creator's profile.** A profile has a Comics tab that lists that person's comics.

The genres are Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Mystery, Romance, Sci Fi, Slice Of Life, Thriller and Other. They appear in capitals on the buttons.

Open a comic to see its overview page: the description, the chapter list, and counts for unique readers, total chapter reads, followers and Buzz tipped (each count shows once it is above zero).

## Read a comic {#read-comics}

1. Open the comic and choose **Start Reading**, or pick a chapter from the list. A chapter with a lock badge and a Buzz price is in early access and is locked, see [Early access chapters](#early-access-chapters).
2. Read. A control in the reader's header switches between **Page view** and **Scroll view**. Use whichever you like.
3. To move to another chapter: in Scroll view use **Next Chapter** at the bottom. In Page view use the arrows at the edges of the page, the arrow keys, or the chapter dropdown in the header. Narrow screens, including phones, always use Scroll view. Page view is the default on a computer.

The reader's header also has a button that shows or hides the comments. On a computer, **Copy link**, **Report comic** (signed-in readers other than the owner) and a download icon are icon buttons. The download icon appears when the creator allows downloads, and opens a "Download as" menu with PDF and CBZ. On a phone they sit in a three-dot menu with Copy link, Download CBZ, Download PDF and Report. The download icon needs download access, which the creator's setting, owners and moderators have. The Page view and Scroll view switch is two icons with those tooltips, and it is not shown on phones. The comments button is a speech-bubble icon.

### Follow, tip and comment {#follow-tip-comment}

On a comic's overview page:

- **Follow.** When you are signed in, the bell button has the tooltip "Get notified of new chapters". Press it again (tooltip "Unfollow comic") to stop.
- **Tip.** The lightning-bolt badge, which shows the total Buzz tipped, is where you send Buzz to the creator. It shows when you are signed in and it is not your own comic. Tips come from Green Buzz on civitai.com and Yellow Buzz on civitai.red. See [Buzz Colors: Blue, Yellow and Green](/buzz-colors-blue-yellow-and-green/).
- **Share** is a copy-link icon and a share icon that opens a post on X. A flag icon reports the comic (signed in, not your own comic). Both are on the comic's overview page. Chapters also have a comment thread.

### Early access chapters {#early-access-chapters}

A creator can put a chapter in **early access**. It costs Buzz to read for a set number of days, and then it becomes free for everyone. In the chapter list it shows a lock badge with the price and "Early access · free in N days". Opening it shows "This chapter is in early access. It will become free in N days." You need to be signed in with enough Buzz to unlock it. Choose **Unlock for N Buzz**. The Buzz you pay goes to the comic's creator. You pay in the Buzz of the site you are on: Green on civitai.com, Yellow on civitai.red. You can't buy your own chapter, and you pay once per chapter.

## Make a comic {#make-a-comic}

On a computer, hover over **Create** in the site header and choose **Create a Comic**. On a phone, go straight to [civitai.com/comics/create](https://civitai.com/comics/create). If you are signed out, you are sent to log in first.

### Create the project {#create-the-project}

The **Create Comic Project** page asks for:

- **Project name** (required).
- **Genre** (optional).
- **Description** (optional, up to 5,000 characters). It shows on the overview page and helps readers find your comic.
- **Hero Banner** (optional): a wide 16:9 image for the overview page. Upload one, pick one from the generator or generate one.
- **Cover** (optional): a portrait image for the browse cards. The same three options apply.

The comic is saved as a draft and stays private to you until you publish a chapter. You can change these later in Project Settings (upload an image or pick one from the generator). After you create it, you land on the project page. On a first visit in a browser, a "Welcome to Comic Creator (Beta)!" dialog explains the Beta.

**Project Settings** (the gear icon) holds more choices:

- **Generation Model**: the AI model used for panels. The default is Nano Banana 2. Each model has different strengths, image limits and sizes.
- **Allow Downloads**: lets readers download the comic as PDF or CBZ.
- **Reading direction**: Left-to-right or Right-to-left (Manga).

### References: keep your characters consistent {#references}

A **reference** is a set of images of a character, location, item or style that the generator uses so it stays the same from panel to panel.

1. In the **References** sidebar, choose the **+** icon, or the **Add Reference** button shown while the list is empty. This opens the Add Reference page, and saving returns you to the project. The download icon, "Import from other projects", brings in references you made elsewhere.
2. Upload 1 to 10 images. The page tips: a clear, front-facing view, the same subject in every image, different angles, consistent lighting and high resolution.
3. Enter a **Reference name**, for example "Maya", and pick a **Reference type**: Character, Location, Style or Item.
4. Choose **Add Reference**. Creating a reference is free.

Later, type **@** and the name, such as "@Maya on a rooftop", in a prompt to include that reference.

### Chapters and panels {#chapters-and-panels}

A new project starts with a draft **Chapter 1**. Choose **Add Chapter** for more. Your chapters are listed in the sidebar. Each shows a colored dot (hover it for Draft, Scheduled, Published or Early Access), its panel count and a gear icon for chapter settings. Open a chapter and choose the large **+** tile at the end of the panel grid to add a panel. The **Create Panel** dialog has four tabs:

- **Generate.** Describe the scene ("Describe the scene... Use @Name to include references"), choose the Generation Model, the aspect ratio (portrait 3:4 works best for most panels) and the number of images per generation (1 to 4), then generate. Generate more than one image to pick the best one.
- **Enhance.** Turn an existing image into a comic panel, with an optional prompt for changes.
- **Bulk Add.** Add several panels at once, each with its own prompt.
- **Import.** Choose images from your generator history to use as panels.

Panels are paid with Blue Buzz or your site's color (see [Buzz Colors: Blue, Yellow and Green](/buzz-colors-blue-yellow-and-green/)). If you have no Buzz, the button opens a purchase window. The generate button shows what the action costs before you confirm. Costs depend on the model and the number of images. If the dialog says your generation queue is full or that generation is unavailable, wait for jobs to finish or try again later.

You can reorder panels by dragging them. On a wide screen, hover a panel card to show its three-dot menu, which has options such as View Details, Iterative Edit, Change Image, Regenerate, Duplicate, Insert after and Delete. Some appear only when they apply, for example Change Image needs more than one candidate image. When you generate more than one image, the card says "images ready, click to choose". Open it and choose **Confirm Selection**.

### Smart Create: let AI plan a chapter {#smart-create}

If your project has at least one finished reference, a **Smart Create** button appears under your chapter list.

1. Enter a **Chapter name** and describe the story or scene. Use @Name to bring in characters.
2. Set the **Number of panels** (2 to 20 per chapter), or leave it empty to let the AI decide.
3. Choose **Plan Panels**. The AI writes a prompt for each panel.
4. On the review step ("N panels planned") edit any prompt, pick the Generation Model and choose **Create Chapter**.

Both the planning step and the creation step show their Buzz cost on the button.

### Publish a chapter {#publish-a-chapter}

A draft chapter needs at least one panel before it can be published. Until then, the Publish button is disabled and its tooltip reads "Add panels before publishing". Use **Preview** to see it as readers will.

1. Choose **Publish**. The dialog is titled **Publish Chapter**.
2. Pick **Publish now** or **Schedule for later** and set the date and time.
3. Optional: turn on **Enable Early Access** (below).
4. Confirm with **Publish** or **Schedule**.

A published chapter can be unpublished from the same button, which reads **Unpublish** (**Cancel Schedule** for a scheduled chapter) and asks you to confirm. The chapter goes back to draft and is no longer visible to readers. If any reader has bought early access to the chapter, Civitai refuses with "This chapter cannot be unpublished because readers have purchased early access to it." A scheduled chapter can be canceled. New chapters notify the people who follow your comic. After a non-scheduled publish, a "Chapter published!" message appears and the live chapter opens in a new tab, unless your browser blocks the popup. Panels you just generated may show the orange **Review** badge for a short while until their scan finishes.

### Early access for your chapters {#early-access-for-creators}

Early access lets you charge Buzz for a chapter for a limited time.

- **Buzz Price:** 100 to 10,000 Buzz.
- **Early Access Period:** how long the chapter stays locked. After it, the chapter is free.
- **Requirements:** early access unlocks at a Creator Score of 10,000. Your score also limits how many days you can pick and how many chapters can be in early access at once. At 10,000 the limit is 3 days and 1 chapter. At 65,000 it is 5 days and 2 chapters, and it rises with your score from there. When your score limits the days, the publish dialog shows "Up to N days at your current score". If you are at your limit of chapters in early access, you see a message when you confirm.
- After publishing, you can lower the price and the period (the period counts from the original publish date), switch early access off, or switch it on in **Chapter Settings**. You can't raise either. The gear icon in the chapter's status badge (**Edit Early Access**) shows while early access is active.

Creator Score is a number Civitai calculates from your activity and how others engage with your work. It is shown on your account page.

### Content and moderation {#content-and-moderation}

- Some generation models make safe-for-work images only. On civitai.com, mature panels can't be viewed or published. A generated panel that comes back mature shows "This generation can only be viewed on civitai.red". On civitai.red, a mature panel you generated is held until you press **Unlock**, which costs Yellow Buzz. Some are capped at a lower rating and can't be unlocked. On civitai.red, uploaded or imported images can be mature, but if an image is classified as mature and Civitai can't verify that it was AI-generated, it is blocked from publishing until you add its generation details (prompt, sampler, steps and other settings). The panel card shows a yellow "Add Metadata" badge, and the panel's detail view has **Add generation details**.
- A chapter can be hidden from readers while a panel waits for moderator review, or if it is flagged as a Terms of Service violation. The chapter list shows a **Review** or **TOS** badge, and the tooltip explains which. A chapter waiting for review publishes automatically when it clears.

## Worked example

You want to publish a short comic about two cats on a rooftop.

1. Go to civitai.com/comics/create. Name it "Rooftop Cats", pick the Comedy genre, add a one-line description (you can also upload or generate a cover), then choose **Create Project**.
2. In **References**, choose **Add Reference** (the **+** button once you have some). Upload three images of your main cat from different angles. Name it "Whiskers", choose type **Character** and save.
3. Your new project already has a draft **Chapter 1**. Open it and choose the **+** tile at the end of the panel grid. On the **Generate** tab, write "@Whiskers on a rooftop at sunset, looking at the city lights". Keep the portrait aspect ratio, set images per generation to 2, check the cost on the button and generate. When the panel says "2 images ready", open it, pick the better image and choose **Confirm Selection**.
4. Add two more panels the same way, changing the scene each time.
5. Press **Preview** to read it through, then **Publish**, choose **Publish now** and confirm.

Your project stays private until you publish a chapter. After that, readers can find "Rooftop Cats" under the Comedy genre and follow it for new chapters, though it can take a short while to show up in browse and search. To put a later chapter in early access for Buzz, use **Enable Early Access** in its publish dialog if your Creator Score allows it.

## Troubleshooting

- **I can't find Create a Comic.** On a computer, hover over the Create button in the header. On a phone, open civitai.com/comics/create directly. If the Create button is grayed out, your account is muted.
- **Smart Create is missing.** It appears once your project has at least one reference and it has finished saving. Add a reference first.
- **Publish is disabled.** The chapter has no panels yet. Add at least one.
- **"Early access ... unlocks at a Creator Score of 10,000."** You are below the minimum. The dialog shows your score.
- **My chapter is hidden from readers.** Check the **Review** or **TOS** badge on the chapter. Review clears on its own, while a TOS flag means you should remove the offending content or contact support.
- **My upload was blocked as mature.** If the image is classified as mature and its generation data can't be verified, add the prompt, sampler, steps and other generation details to the image, then try again.
- **Panel generation is queued or unavailable.** The dialog says whether your queue is full or generation is down. Wait and try again.
- **A panel shows Failed.** Press **Regenerate** on the card to try again. A failed card always shows "Buzz has been refunded", and that is reliable when the message says the generation failed, was canceled or expired. If the message says the images failed to download or the upload failed, check your Buzz balance before pressing Regenerate. Refunds are not issued for other Beta-related issues, so check the cost shown before you confirm.
