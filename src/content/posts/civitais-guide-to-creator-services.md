Creator Services lets you commission custom work from a creator on Civitai, such as a LoRA trained for you or a music video. The request, the payment, the files and the conversation all happen on Civitai, so neither side has to rely on DMs and payment apps elsewhere. The launch announcement is [Creator Services: Commission a LoRA, a Music Video, or Whatever You Need](https://civitai.com/articles/38740).

The short version: **your Buzz is held in escrow, not paid, until you accept the delivery.**

## How a deal works {#how-a-deal-works}

1. **Find a service.** Open [Services](https://civitai.com/services) (also **Browse Services** in your user menu), or look on a creator's storefront or in the Shop section of their profile. Each service shows its price, how many days it takes and how many revisions are included.
2. **Send a request.** Describe what you want and attach reference files. You pay in Buzz when you send the request. The Buzz is held in escrow, so nothing goes to the creator yet. The deal shows as **Awaiting seller**.
3. **The creator accepts.** The deal becomes **In progress**. You can talk it through in the deal chat, and you are notified as it moves along.
4. **Check the preview.** Most services include a preview, which the creator uploads along with the final files. If there is one, you see a low-resolution, watermarked copy, and the final files stay sealed until you accept the delivery. If it is not right, use **Request a revision**. You can ask for as many revisions as the service includes.
5. **Accept the delivery.** When you are happy, accept it. That completes the deal and the final files are yours. If you never respond, the deal completes on its own after a few days.

Everything about a deal, including its status, chat, files and updates, is on [your deals page](https://civitai.com/services/deals).

**Save your files.** Deal files are deleted 30 days after a deal is declined, expires or is canceled, and 90 days after a completed deal's dispute window ends. Download anything you want to keep.

## Your Buzz and escrow {#escrow}

Escrow means the platform holds your Buzz while the work is done, instead of handing it to the creator up front. The deal page says it plainly: the Buzz is held, not paid, and nothing moves until the deal completes or is canceled. That protects you if the work never arrives, and it protects the creator, who knows the Buzz is there before starting.

Right now there is no fee for escrow or moderation.

## When the creator gets paid {#creator-paid}

Right now, the creator's Buzz is released 48 hours after you accept, which gives you time to try the files and report a problem. We're adding reviews, so soon you'll be able to leave one on the deal page, and a happy review pays the creator right away. If you don't leave one, they're paid after the 48 hours as usual.

## Canceling and refunds {#canceling}

- **Before the creator accepts:** use **Withdraw request** to cancel and get your Buzz back.
- **After the creator accepts:** use **Request cancellation** and the creator can agree to it.
- **If the creator is running late:** once a deal is a little past its due date you can use **Cancel (overdue)** yourself. A deal that goes well past its due date with nothing delivered is also canceled and refunded automatically.
- **If you cannot agree:** a deal that goes wrong and cannot be settled between you and the creator goes to Civitai moderators.

## Offering a service {#offering-a-service}

Services are open to eligible creators in good standing. The Services tab on your shop's **Manage** page shows whether you qualify, and [Civitai's Guide to Creator Score and the Leaderboard](/civitais-guide-to-creator-score-and-the-leaderboard/) explains how your standing is measured. If you qualify, set up what you offer there. Requests reach you with the Buzz already held, so you know the buyer can pay before you start. You can share a watermarked preview, take revision requests and hand over the final files in the same place.
