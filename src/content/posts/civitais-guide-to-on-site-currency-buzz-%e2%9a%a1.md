## On-Site Currency? Buzz?

<div class="columns">

<div class="column">

**Buzz** is the [Civitai](https://civitai.com) on-site currency!

From using generative AI features and collaborating with Creators to tipping fellow Civitans and personalizing your Civitai profile, Buzz can now be used in a variety of useful ways on Civitai.

</div>

</div>

### How do I get Buzz?

There are a number of avenues to obtain Buzz! While direct purchase provides immediate access to Buzz, it’s _**by no means necessary**_. Civitai is designed to ensure its most active, committed, and helpful members are rewarded for their engagement and contributions!

-   **Direct purchase**! Directly purchasing Buzz with a credit card provides immediate access to Buzz! **_Direct purchase applies only to Green Buzz, for PG-level content creation on [Civitai.green](https://civitai.green/)_**
-   Please note that as of May 2025, direct purchase with Credit Card is no longer available on Civitai.com due to restrictions on legal user-generated adult content from Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.

    Yellow Buzz can be purchased with cryptocurrency via direct deposit from any wallet or exchange – please see our [Guide to Purchasing Buzz with Crypto](/civitais-guide-to-purchasing-buzz-with-crypto/) for more details.


-   **Engage** on [Civitai.com](https:\\www.civitai.com)! Free Blue Buzz is awarded by reacting to images, reviews, and comments, and also when other Civitans add your content to collections, or post images to models you’ve uploaded.

-   **Tips** & **transfers**! If other Civitans like your content, they can send a tip! The tip system can also be used to share or transfer Buzz between users.

-   **Contest Rewards**! Participate in our regular contests to earn Buzz.

-   **Bounties**! Submit entries to Bounties via the Bounty Board System.

-   **Creator Compensation** – When resources you’ve uploaded are used by others in the Image Generator, you’ll receive a cut of the generation cost! See your [Buzz Dashboard](https://civitai.com/user/buzz-dashboard) for more information;

-   **Begging**! Submit your begging images to the [Buzz Beggar’s](https://civitai.com/collections/3870938) [Bo](https://civitai.com/collections/3870938)[ard](https://civitai.com/collections/3870938) and you might be tipped by a generous Buzz Benefactor!

-   **More**! We have a number of other Buzz-earning avenues planned.

**New** users will receive **100 Blue Buzz** when they create their account.

### Purchasing Buzz

Should you wish to buy Buzz, it can be purchased from the [Buy Buzz](https://civitai.com/purchase/buzz) button in the main profile-picture menu, at the top right of the screen, or via a number of other links around the site, including on the Buzz Dashboard.

### What can I do with Buzz?

We will be gradually integrating Buzz into site features and actions, but right now you can use Buzz to;

-   **Tip your favorite creators**! Share your Buzz with other creators to show your appreciation of their work.

-   **Post Bounties**! Need a service, but don’t have the time or knowledge to achieve it? Post a Bounty with a Buzz reward and have the Community help you out.

-   **Train a LoRA**! You can spend your **Blue, Yellow, or Green** **Buzz** to train LoRA with the [Civitai on-site LoRA Trainer](/using-civitai-the-on-site-lora-trainer/)! The LoRA trainer is a great way to create your own models if you don’t have the required hardware, or know-how, to train locally. Now, with a little Buzz, you can let our infrastructure do the hard work – creating anything you can imagine, via a simple wizard!

-   Create images with the [Civitai Image Generator](/using-civitai-the-on-site-image-generator/)! Images are priced on a sliding Buzz scale, based upon Step Count and model architecture.

## Types of Buzz

Buzz is split into three distinct types, each with their own rules for earning and usage. The key difference is in how they’re acquired and where they can be spent. When interacting with Buzz across the platform, pay close attention to the color-coding: **Blue** and **Yellow**, and **Green**.

These visual cues will help you quickly identify which type of Buzz you’re working with, whether you’re earning it through activities or spending it in the generator. Understanding the distinctions between these types ensures you make the most of your Buzz!

-   **Yellow Buzz** – Yellow Buzz is your primary currency for Civitai.com platform transactions and NSFW image creation. It can be accrued via;
    -   Purchases via cryptocurrency
    -   Tips from other creators
    -   Winning Bounty Submissions
    -   Participating in the Creator Compensation program (rewards for users using your content in the Generator)
    -   Winning Civitai Contests

-   **Blue Buzz** – Blue Buzz is your safe-for-work\* “creation” currency, and can be earned in various ways including;
    -   Awarded to new accounts upon creation
    -   “Passive” community rewards (e.g. users reacting to your content)
    -   “Active” community rewards (e.g. posting images, following new users)
    -   Claimable once daily from the Image Generator
    -   Earned [for each ad impression you scroll past](/civitais-guide-to-ads-2/) while browsing the site

**\*Note that Blue Buzz is primarily a safe-for-work Currency. It can be used on Civitai.com and Civitai.green to generate PG, and PG-13 level content.**

**_If you have a Civitai.com Membership, you can also use Blue Buzz for NSFW generation on Civitai.com._**

-   **Green Buzz** – Green Buzz is the primary currency for [Civitai.green](https://civitai.green/) platform transactions and SFW image creation. It can be accrued via;
    -   Direct Credit or Debit Card purchases
    -   Tips from other creators
    -   Participating in the Civitai.green Creator Compensation program (rewards for users using your content in the Generator)

All types of Buzz can be used to generate images and train LoRAs on-site, but **Blue Buzz can only be used for these functions**, and _cannot be traded between Civitai accounts_.
