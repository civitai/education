Showcases let you choose what your profile shows first. Before, your profile led with your most popular models and articles, which is not always your best work. Now you can add your own sections, give each a title, and fill them with the work you want people to see. The launch announcement is [Showcase Sections: Pick What Your Profile Shows First](https://civitai.com/articles/39087). It is the first piece of the profile facelift introduced in [this article](https://civitai.com/articles/38570).

## What you can do {#what-you-can-do}

- Add up to **10 showcases** to your profile, each with its own title.
- Put up to **32 images, models or articles** in each one, in the order you choose. Each showcase holds one of the three.
- Add items from the item's own menu with **Add to Showcase**.
- Replace the "View all" button with your own label and link.
- Drag showcases into place or hide them, like your other profile sections.

## Make a showcase {#make-a-showcase}

1. Open **Edit profile** and go to **Page sections**.
2. Press **Add showcase section**.
3. Choose whether it holds images, models or articles, and give it a title.
4. Pick items. The search only returns your own work of that type.
5. Drag the items to put them in order, then save.

## Add as you go {#add-as-you-go}

You do not have to build a showcase in one sitting. On any of your images, models or published articles, open the menu and choose **Add to Showcase**. You see each of your showcases of that type and how full it is. The Showcase at the top of your profile is also in the list, for images and models. Pick one and the item is added.

## Order, hide and move {#order}

- Drag items inside a showcase to reorder them.
- Drag the showcase itself to move it up or down your profile, or switch it off to hide it.
- Viewers see your order. The automatic sections, such as Most popular models, stay wherever you put them.

## Give a showcase a button {#button}

Each showcase can have an optional button where "View all" usually sits. Give it your own label and point it anywhere on Civitai, such as a filtered list of your models, or to your own site with an https link. A link that leaves Civitai shows the usual heads-up before someone goes.

## Good to know {#good-to-know}

- An empty showcase stays hidden.
- Anything deleted, unpublished or filtered out for a viewer drops out of a showcase, the same as in the Showcase at the top of your profile.
- Your current Showcase stays as it was, and it is still in the Add to Showcase list.
