A **storyline** is a story told in posts. Each chapter is an ordinary Civitai post of images. The person who starts the storyline writes the main path, and other creators can continue it from the end of any chapter. The story grows into a tree of alternatives, and readers choose which branch to follow.

This guide covers how to read a storyline, how to write the next chapter of someone else's, how to start your own, and how to manage who can continue it.

## The words you will see {#words}

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Storyline** | The whole story, with its title, its chapters and its branches. |
| **Chapter** | One post in the storyline. |
| **Main path** | The chapters the storyline's creator wrote. |
| **Fork** | A chapter that more than one chapter continues from. Each continuation is a branch. |
| **Map** | A sheet that shows the path you are reading, the main-path chapters ahead of it and the forks near it. |
| **Collaborator** | Someone the creator has endorsed. Their continuations skip review. |

## Read a storyline {#read}

### Find one {#find}

- Open [civitai.com/storylines](https://civitai.com/storylines) to browse storylines. You can filter the list by **All**, **Ongoing**, **Complete** or **Open for continuations**, and sort it by **Newest** or **Most chapters**.
- Images that belong to a storyline show a small pill in the image feed with the storyline's title and the chapter number, such as "Lantern Road · Ch 2". Select it to open the image in storyline mode.
- In image detail, an image that belongs to a storyline has a **Read the story** chip. Select it to enter storyline mode.
- On a chapter's post page, the storyline pill has a menu with **Read from here in the viewer** and **Story page and map**.
- On a storyline's page, choose **Start reading** to begin at chapter 1. If you can continue the storyline, the page also has a **Continue the story** button. An empty storyline shows **Write chapter 1** to its creator.

### Walk the story {#walk}

In storyline mode, the left and right controls in image detail walk through the images of the chapters on your path. They do not page the feed the way they usually do. A chip in the toolbar names the storyline and the chapter you are in.

On the last image, the right arrow or the **What happens next** button opens the end of the chapter, where you choose how to continue. The left arrow closes it again. On a computer, when the chapter has only one way on, pressing the right arrow again continues into it. When the story branches, the arrows never move you into a chapter you have not chosen. Each way on shows its first image and the share of readers who took it. The first way on gets the large card. It is labeled **Keep reading** and has a **Read chapter** button. It is the creator's chapter if there is one. Continuations by the creator's endorsed collaborators come next, then everyone else's, ordered by how many readers took them. The other paths are listed under the **Other paths** heading.

:::note
If you are the first reader to reach a fork, you see **Be the first to choose**. Once readers have chosen, each way on shows its share.
:::

### Use the map {#map}

Select the chip in the toolbar to open the map. It slides down from the chip, and on a phone it is a bottom sheet. The image stays visible behind it.

The map shows:

- one list of chapters: the path you are on, each row tagged with its chapter number and **You are here** where you are, followed by the main-path chapters ahead of you tagged **Canon ahead**,
- under some chapters, **Other paths after chapter N**, the branches that start near where you are, and
- **Another branch joins here** rows where two branches meet.

It also has a **Read chapter** button for the next chapter. At the end of your path it shows how readers split, and **Be the first to choose** if nobody has chosen yet. You can jump to the chapters shown on the map. **Leave the storyline · browse this post's images** takes you back to browsing the post's images, and **Story page** opens the storyline's page.

### Branches can rejoin {#rejoin}

Two branches can meet again at one chapter. Your path and the chapter numbers follow the way you came. The link in your address bar carries your route, so if you share it, the other person reads along the same path.

### Captions {#captions}

An author can add a caption to each image of a chapter. A caption shows as a note at the bottom of the image. If it is long, tap it to expand it, and tap again to collapse it. The X button on the note (**Hide image description**) hides captions, and a small speech-bubble button (**Show image description**) brings them back. Your choice is remembered in that browser.

### The storyline page {#storyline-page}

The page for a storyline has:

- **Chapters**, with the main path down the left in large tiles. Once the story forks, each other branch runs in its own colored lane beside it, with a band where the story forks and another where a branch joins back. Any chapter opens in storyline mode.
- **Contributors**, with who started the storyline and who continued it.
- A comment thread for the whole storyline.
- **About continuing**, which says whether the storyline is open to new chapters.

### Content levels {#content-levels}

Every storyline has a **maximum rating** that its creator sets. A chapter above it shows only as a placeholder, without its images, so the chapters after it stay reachable. A chapter whose post was removed shows as a placeholder too. Your own browsing level still applies, chapter by chapter. See the Questions below for what each placeholder message means.

## Write the next chapter {#continue}

Anyone signed in can continue a storyline, unless its creator has closed it or limited it to collaborators. A storyline that cannot be continued says so instead of offering the button.

1. Get to the end of the chapter you want to continue from. You can fork from the end of any chapter, not only the last one. To continue an earlier chapter, open that chapter's post page, or enter the storyline from that chapter's pill or **Read the story** chip, so that the chapter is the end of your path. Then choose **What happens next** on its last image, or use the end-of-chapter card on the post page.
2. Under **Write what happens next**, select **Write the next chapter**. The post editor opens already knowing which chapter you are continuing.
3. Make your post as you normally would. You can add a caption to each image in the box under it, which asks "What happens in this picture? (optional)". Captions are optional, up to 500 characters, and the box has a **Preview** toggle. A caption saves when you click out of the box, and the box shows **Saved** or **Not saved**. Clearing the box removes the caption.
4. Publish the post. The button reads **Publish and submit** for contributors and **Publish chapter N** for the creator. The post is submitted as the next chapter.

If you already made a post that fits, choose **or use a post you already made** instead, pick the post and choose **Submit** (the creator's button reads **Add chapter**).

What happens next depends on who you are:

- **An endorsed collaborator:** your chapter is added right away, with no review.
- **Anyone else:** your chapter waits for the creator to review it. You see that it was submitted and is waiting for approval.
- **The creator:** your chapter is added once it is published and its images are rated.

A contributor's continuation that is waiting for review expires if the creator does not answer within 7 days. Endorsed collaborators' chapters normally skip the queue, and a chapter from a collaborator is added once its post has finished rating and publishing.

A post rated above the storyline's maximum rating cannot be submitted. If you are not the creator, a post that is still a draft, scheduled or still being rated can be submitted now, and it reaches the creator once it is published and rated. If you are the creator, the post must already be published and rated. A post that is still waiting on a review cannot be submitted yet.

Your continuation credits the earlier chapters to their authors. Nothing is copied, and earlier chapters stay theirs.

### Join an existing chapter {#join}

The creator can also bring two branches back together. At the end of a chapter, choose **Join an existing chapter**, the link next to the **Write the next chapter** button in the **Write what happens next** box. A window titled **Add the next chapter** opens, with a switch between **Use a post you made** and **Join an existing chapter**. The picker only offers chapters that can be joined. The first chapter is never offered. Chapters that would loop the story back, that skip chapters along a route, or that already continue from this one are not offered. A chapter can be joined from at most 8 chapters. Choose **Join this chapter** to finish.

## Start your own storyline {#start}

You can start a storyline in two ways.

**From the Storylines page**

1. Go to [civitai.com/storylines](https://civitai.com/storylines) and choose **Start a storyline**.
2. Enter a **Title**, set the **Max rating** and choose **Who can continue**.
3. Choose **Start storyline**. The post editor opens with the storyline already attached.
4. Publish your post. It becomes chapter 1. Until then the storyline is empty, and only you can see it.

**From a post you already published**

1. Open the post's menu and choose **Make this chapter 1 of a storyline**.
2. In the **Start a storyline** window, enter a **Title** and choose **Who can continue**. Max rating levels below the post's rating are unavailable, because the post becomes chapter 1.
3. Choose **Start storyline**.

If you leave the editor before publishing, the storyline stays empty and you can add its first chapter later. Open the storyline's page and choose **Use a post you've published**, then **Make it chapter 1**. If the post's rating is above the storyline's maximum rating, the maximum rises to fit it.

The post must be rated first. If you see a message that the post has not been rated yet, try again once its images have been scanned.

### Who can continue it {#who-can-continue}

You choose one of two settings, and you can change it later from the gear (**Manage**) button on your storyline's page:

| Setting | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Anyone, I review first** | Anyone signed in can submit a continuation. You review each one. |
| **Only collaborators** | Only people you endorse can continue it. |

## Manage your storyline {#manage}

Select the gear (**Manage**) button on your storyline's page. Only the creator sees it.

- **Story settings** and **Collaborators** are the two tabs of the window. Story settings also lets you edit the title.
- **Covers:** a **Card cover**, shown in the storyline list, and a **Banner** across the top of the storyline's page. Pick a picture from your chapters or your endorsed collaborators' chapters, or upload one. Without a banner, the page shows the card cover, blurred.
- **Status:** **Ongoing** or **Complete**. Complete closes the storyline to new continuations.
- **Who can continue** and **Maximum rating**, as above. Chapters rated above the maximum are hidden, and higher-rated continuations cannot be submitted.
- **Collaborators:** **Endorse** a person by username. Their continuations skip review and are listed after the main path at the end of a chapter. Removing someone keeps their chapters in the story. It stops the auto-approval and moves their chapters out of the collaborators' group at the end of a chapter.

To delete a storyline, open the **More options** (⋯) menu on its page and choose **Delete storyline**, then confirm. This removes the storyline, its chapter links and its discussion. Every chapter's post stays published as an ordinary post.

### Review continuations {#review}

Continuations from people you have not endorsed wait for your review. Open the **Placements** page at [civitai.com/user/placements](https://civitai.com/user/placements) and choose **Storylines** at the top. **Received** shows the continuations waiting for you, and **Sent** shows the ones you submitted to other storylines. For each received one you can:

- **Accept** it, which adds it to the story.
- **Endorse** it, which accepts it and also endorses its author as a collaborator.
- **Decline** it.

A continuation rated above your storyline's maximum rating cannot be accepted. A continuation you do not answer expires after 7 days.

## A worked example {#worked-example}

Maya reads a storyline called "Lantern Road" and wants to take it somewhere new.

1. She opens the storyline from the Storylines page and chooses **Start reading**.
2. She reads to the end of chapter 3 and chooses **What happens next**. She sees two paths. The main path has most of the readers. She opens the map to see where readers went.
3. She wants a different turn, so she chooses **Write the next chapter** under **Write what happens next**.
4. In the editor she uploads her images, adds a caption to each one and publishes the post.
5. Maya is not endorsed, so she sees that her chapter was submitted and is waiting for approval.
6. The creator opens the **Placements** page, finds her chapter under **Storylines**, and chooses **Accept**. Maya's chapter appears as a new branch at the end of chapter 3.

If the creator had chosen **Endorse** instead, Maya would be an endorsed collaborator, and her later chapters would be added without review.

## If something looks wrong {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| I do not see **Write the next chapter** | The storyline may be complete or closed to new chapters, or limited to collaborators and you are not endorsed. A badge such as **Closed to new chapters** or **Collaborators only** appears in place of **or use a post you already made**. On the storyline page, a chip shows **Closed** or **Collaborators only**, and a complete storyline shows **Complete**. You also cannot continue from a chapter that shows as a placeholder. |
| My post cannot be submitted | A post rated above the storyline's maximum rating cannot be submitted. A post that is waiting in review cannot be submitted yet, so try again later. A post that is already a chapter in a storyline, or that you already submitted to this storyline, is refused. You can also have only a limited number of continuations waiting with the same creator at once, so wait for an answer first. |
| A join was refused | The message says why: the chapter comes before this one so the story would loop, the link would skip chapters on a route through the story, the chapter already continues from this one, or a chapter can be joined from at most 8 chapters. |
| I cannot find the gear (**Manage**) button | Only the storyline's creator sees it. |
| I cannot find the review queue | Open [civitai.com/user/placements](https://civitai.com/user/placements) and choose **Storylines** at the top. |

## Questions {#questions}

**Can I write the next chapter of any storyline?**
Anyone signed in can, unless the creator closed it, marked it complete, or limited it to collaborators.

**Can I continue from a chapter that is not the last one?**
Yes. You can fork from the end of any chapter. Open that chapter's post page, or enter the storyline from that chapter, and use the end of the chapter there.

**Why do I see a placeholder instead of a chapter?**
You will see one of these messages: this chapter was deleted, this chapter was removed, this chapter is not available, this chapter is rated above the storyline's maximum, or this chapter is hidden by your browsing level. "Not available" also covers chapters by people you have hidden or blocked, and chapters that are not published or rated yet.

**Where do I report a storyline?**
Use the menu on the storyline's page to report the storyline, for example its title. Report a chapter as you would any post or image.

## I need more help {#help}

If a storyline is not behaving the way this guide describes, contact Civitai through the [support portal](https://civitai.com/support).
