## What is the Cosmetic Shop?

The Civitai Cosmetic Shop is a fun way to elevate the aesthetics of your profile, ensuring you stand out from the crowd of other Creators. Offering a range of cosmetic options, you can customize the appearance of your profile, and it’s not just about looking great— it’s about making a statement and highlighting your work!

Cosmetics are available to purchase with [Buzz ?](/civitais-guide-to-buzz-%e2%9a%a1/), and are split into four categories;

## FAQ & Notes {#requirements-limitations}

-   What is Boring-Ass Profile Syndrome? If you don’t have any cosmetics, you’ve got it. Happily, the cure is only a few Buzz away!

-   Cosmetics are _generally_ non-refundable purchases. Sometimes odd situations do occur when purchasing, and in those cases there might be a valid refund reason. Reach out to us at support@civitai.com and we’ll evaluate the situation on a case-by-case basis.

-   Why are some items sold out? Some items will be **exclusives**; limited run items, only available for a specific time, or until a specific quantity are sold. These exclusives will not return for sale at a later date.

-   Cosmetics are **not tradeable or transferrable**. Cosmetics must remain on the account on which they were purchased.

-   The store will be refreshed with new content often, but not on any set schedule. Use the notification bell feature to be alerted to new content drops!
