![Illustration of a shop stall with a badge, an avatar frame, a profile banner and three stickers](/images/guides/creator-shop-and-stickers/creator-shop-cover.webp)

Civitai has two kinds of shop. The **Shop** at [civitai.com/shop](https://civitai.com/shop), also called the Cosmetic Shop, sells cosmetics and stickers to everyone, and it includes items made by community creators. Each creator can also run their own **Creator Shop** on their profile, where they sell items they designed and keep most of the Buzz from each sale.

This guide covers both sides, and replaces the older [Guide to the Cosmetic Shop](/civitais-guide-to-the-cosmetic-shop/). If you want to buy, start with [What you can buy](#what-you-can-buy). If you want to sell, go to [Selling in your own shop](#selling). Stickers have their own section, because they work differently from every other item: you spend them a use at a time.

## What you can buy {#what-you-can-buy}

Everything in the shop is priced in Buzz. The shop filters list the cosmetic types, and creators can sell four of them:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Badge** | A small badge shown on your profile. |
| **Avatar Frame** (avatar decoration) | An animated or static frame around your profile picture. |
| **Profile Background** | A background behind your mini-profile card, on your profile, and on model and image pages. |
| **Sticker** | An image you can put in comments and chat, and place on other people's images. See [Stickers](#stickers). |

Civitai also sells its own items, such as content frames (shown as Content Decoration in the filters), which dress up a model, image or article. A content frame can only be applied to one piece of content at a time.
You can also buy **packs**. A pack is a bundle of items sold at one price. A pack card says "Pack of 6" and so on, and opening it shows what is inside.

Some items are **limited**: the creator set a quantity or a time window, and a card with a quantity shows how many are left. When a limited item sells out, it can't be bought unless the creator raises the quantity.

## Buying an item {#buying}

1. Open [civitai.com/shop](https://civitai.com/shop) from the top menu, or open a creator's profile and go to its **Shop** tab. A profile only has a Shop tab when its owner has made the shop public.
2. Browse. The shop can be sorted by Newest, Oldest, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low, Most Popular and Name (A-Z). **Filters** let you pick cosmetic types, and the chips **Owned**, **Hide Owned**, **Wishlisted**, **Limited** and **Accepts Blue Buzz** narrow the list further. The search box also finds a sticker by its name or by the name you type between colons, such as `:wave:`.
3. Click **Preview** on an item. The preview shows the item as it will look on your profile.
4. If the item accepts Blue Buzz, choose how to pay with the **Pay with** menu. See [Which Buzz pays](#which-buzz-pays).
5. Click **Purchase**. Your Buzz is charged right away, and there is no second confirmation step. Check the price and the Pay with choice first.

After a purchase, the item is yours. Badges, frames and backgrounds can be applied immediately from the window that opens, or later from your profile. A sticker goes into your sticker picker.

Some points to know:

- **Items you own are marked.** You can't buy a badge, avatar frame or profile background twice. Content frames and stickers can be bought again, and buying a sticker you own adds more uses. A sticker you hold with unlimited uses can't be bought again.
- **New items.** The shop changes often, with no fixed schedule. The bell on the shop page turns notifications for new items on or off.
- **A creator can't buy their own non-sticker items.** If you made a cosmetic, you already own it.
- **Blocked users.** If either of you has blocked the other, the item isn't available to you.
- **If something goes wrong.** If a moderator pulls an item after it sold, for example for copied artwork, every buyer is refunded automatically. For anything else, write to support (see [I need more help](#help)).

### Buying packs {#buying-packs}

A pack's price covers all its items. If the pack contains cosmetics you already own that the pack's creator made, the pack detail shows what you owe after a discount for those. Items made by other creators are not discounted, and items you made yourself are not charged. The button reads "Purchase pack". Items that are consumable, meaning stickers, are never discounted, because buying a sticker you own adds uses. A pack can't be bought if one of its items is no longer available, because it could not deliver everything it was built with.

## Which Buzz pays {#which-buzz-pays}

Buzz comes in three colors, and which one pays depends on the site you are on and on what the creator chose. [Buzz Colors: Blue, Yellow and Green](/buzz-colors-blue-yellow-and-green/) explains each color. In the shop, it works like this:

- **Your site's color always works.** On civitai.com that is Green Buzz. On civitai.red it is Yellow Buzz. Yellow Buzz can't be spent on civitai.com.
- **Blue Buzz works only when the creator allows it.** Each item has an "Accepts Blue Buzz" setting. When it is on, the purchase window shows a **Pay with** menu. You can pay the full price in your site's color, or choose **Blue + Green** (on civitai.red, Blue + Yellow), which uses your Blue Buzz first and pays the rest in the paid color. The shop's **Accepts Blue Buzz** filter shows only items that accept Blue.
- **When you pay in Blue, the creator is paid in Blue** for that part of the sale.
- **Placing a sticker on an image**, and buying a use from the placement panel, are paid in your site's color only. Blue is not accepted there.
- **Buying more uses of a sticker** from the sticker picker pays in your site's color only. Blue works when you buy the sticker again from the shop and its creator accepts Blue.

Selling has its own Buzz colors: the creator's submission fee can be paid from Yellow, Green or Blue, and the creator is paid in the color the buyer used. See [How you are paid](#how-you-are-paid).

## Applying what you bought {#applying}

Cosmetics are applied on your profile:

- On the shop page, **Customize profile** opens the profile editor, where you pick your badge, frame and background.
- To see how many uses each sticker has left, open the **Sticker Book** tab on your profile (only you see the counts, and an infinity symbol means unlimited). The sticker picker in the comment editor also shows the count on each sticker.
- Content frames are applied to a model, image or article from that item, one at a time.

A cosmetic made by a creator can only be worn once Civitai has approved it. If a moderator later asks the creator for changes or pulls the item, it comes off every profile that was wearing it.

## Stickers {#stickers}

A sticker is a small image, often animated, that you can drop into a comment, a chat message, or onto someone else's image. A sticker has two numbers on its shop card:

- **Uses included**: how many times you can use the sticker after buying it. A card can read, for example, "100 uses included · 5 Buzz per extra use". Older stickers can read "Unlimited uses".
- **Buzz per extra use**: the price of one more use when you run out. The creator sets it, and it can't be below 5 Buzz.

### Using stickers in comments and chat {#stickers-in-comments}

- In a comment, click the smiley-face **Insert sticker** button in the comment editor. Each sticker you add to a comment costs one use. Editing a comment doesn't charge you again for a sticker that was already in it.
- In chat (direct messages), you can choose the **Stickers** tab in the **Insert sticker** picker or type the sticker's name between colons, such as `:party_cat:`. Stickers in chat are free and don't use up your uses, but you still need to own the sticker.
- If you try to send a sticker you have no uses left for, you are told to remove it or buy more.

### Buying more uses {#top-ups}

When you run out, you can buy more uses of a sticker you own. Open the sticker picker and click a sticker that is out of uses (it appears dimmed). The purchase window asks "Uses to buy" and has a **Buy uses** button. You pay the sticker's price per use for each one, up to 1,000 uses at a time. You can only top up a sticker you already own, event stickers can't be topped up, and a sticker you hold with unlimited uses doesn't need it. A sticker from before per-use pricing may say it doesn't sell extra uses. Buy it again in the shop instead.

### Placing a sticker on an image {#placing}

You can also pay to put a sticker on another creator's image, as a small decoration the owner has agreed to.

1. Open an image. Next to the sticker count under the image, click the **+** button (**Place a sticker**). It shows when you are signed in, the image owner allows stickers, and the image isn't yours.
2. The sticker panel lists the stickers you own, with the uses each has left. A spent sticker is dimmed. With a large collection, the panel adds a search box and sorting. You can also browse stickers on sale from the panel and buy one there. Drag a sticker onto the image, then move, resize, rotate or fade it. The owner's settings limit how large a sticker can be, and a sticker can't be made fainter than 30% opacity.
3. Add an optional note of up to 300 characters, then click **Place**. If a free placement is available on that image, a selector appears with **Free** and the price, and **Free** is chosen by default. With Free selected, the button reads **Place free**. Choose the price to pay instead, and the button reads **Place**. A free placement that the owner declines still counts as your free placement for the day. The panel tells you who receives the Buzz you pay. Placing a sticker is paid in your site's color only (Green on civitai.com, Yellow on civitai.red), not Blue. Until the owner approves a pending sticker, only you can see it. A placement also uses one of the sticker's uses. Expiry returns all your Buzz and a decline returns most of it (see below), but the use is not returned. If you drag a spent sticker onto an image, the Place button is replaced by **Buy a use**, which buys one use at the sticker's extra-use price, in your site's color.

You can't place a sticker on your own content, and you can have at most 10 placements waiting with the same creator.

What happens next depends on the image owner's setting:

- **Accept all**: the sticker goes live as soon as you pay.
- **Review each one**: the owner sees it in their queue and approves or declines. If they don't answer within 48 hours, it expires and you get all your Buzz back. If they decline, you get most of it back and a fee stays with the owner.
- **No stickers**: you can't place a sticker on that owner's images.

Once a sticker is live, whether the owner approved it or it was accepted automatically, the owner can't remove it for seven days. After that they can take it off, and a paid placement's Buzz is not returned, because the owner was paid when it went live. If a moderator takes down a sticker's artwork, buyers are refunded what they paid for the sticker, and any placement still waiting for review is refunded in full. A placement that is already live comes down, and the Buzz the owner received stays with them. A moderator removing a placement for abuse does not refund the placer either.

Some stickers are limited by a moderator to mature images only, or to non-mature images only. Such a sticker also can't be placed on an image that hasn't been rated yet. Such stickers don't appear in the panel for those images.

Each person can make **one free placement a day**, once per image, on images whose owners have free slots open. The day resets at midnight UTC. A free placement costs no Buzz, but it still needs a sticker you own and uses one of its uses. A declined or expired placement doesn't give the day back, and you can only ever make one free placement on a given image.

### Letting people place stickers on your work {#owner-settings}

Open your account settings and go to the **Creator** section, then **Stickers on your images**. You can set:

- **No stickers, Review each one, or Accept all.** The default is Review each one.
- **Allow stickers on** Images, Videos, or both.
- **Price per placement.** You choose a price, up to a cap that depends on your creator score and your membership tier.
- **Largest sticker allowed**, as a share of the image width.
- A free-slot control, when your cap allows free placements, and a **Review pending stickers** button.

Placements waiting for you appear on your placements page, at [civitai.com/user/placements](https://civitai.com/user/placements). Stickers can also be turned on or off for a single post or image.

### Your Sticker Book {#sticker-book}

Your profile has a **Sticker Book** tab. It shows the stickers you own, where you placed them, and the stickers other people placed on your work. The owner sees how much Buzz stickers earned on their images. The gear icon opens **Sticker settings**, where you can hide the tab from visitors and choose whether to show the stickers you own. How many uses you have left is only ever shown to you.

## Selling in your own shop {#selling}

### Before you start {#before-you-start}

- **Where your shop is.** Open the **Shop** tab on your own profile. Members of the Creator Program also have a **My Shop** shortcut in the user menu.
- **You pay a submission fee up front**, in Buzz. The form starts on Yellow, so switch **Pay fee with** if your Buzz is Green or Blue. The form shows the amount. Compare it with what one sale earns you, and plan for how many sales it takes to cover it. It is not refunded if the item is rejected.
- **Your artwork must pass automatic checks** and the content standards below.
- **Everything you submit is reviewed** by Civitai before it can be sold.

The **Manage Your Shop** button on your shop page opens the page where you submit and track items.

### What you can sell {#what-you-can-sell}

Badges, Avatar Frames, Profile Backgrounds and Stickers. You can bundle your items, and other creators' items that allow resale, into packs. You can also list other creators' items in your shop when they allow it. See [Letting others sell your item](#resale).

### Artwork requirements {#artwork}

Your artwork is checked automatically before you are charged. Items must be PNG or WebP files of up to 50 MB, and every type except Profile Background needs a transparent background.

| Type | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Badge | At least 144 x 144 px, 1:1 | Built on a hexagon. |
| Avatar Frame | At least 120 x 120 px, 1:1 | At least half of the avatar must stay visible. |
| Profile Background | At least 450 x 144 px, 25:8 | Keep text light. |
| Sticker | 320 to 512 px on the long edge, at most 2:1 either way | Animated stickers: at most 150 frames and 30 frames per second. |

Content rules apply to all items: safe for work, PG or PG-13, your own original design, and no characters, logos or symbols from brands or franchises you don't own. Read the full [Creator Cosmetic Quality Standards](https://civitai.com/content/cosmetic-standards) before you design. [Cosmetic Studio](https://cosmetic-studio.civitai.com/) is a tool for designing cosmetics that meet them. For stickers, the in-app standards add that the artwork must read at 22 px and should avoid words and small text. Duplicate artwork that has already been submitted is refused.

### Submit an item {#submit}

1. Open your shop, then **Manage Your Shop**, then **Submit an item**.
2. Choose the **Cosmetic type**. It can't be changed after you submit.
3. Upload your artwork. The form shows a preview and tells you if a check fails. For an Avatar Frame, **Adjust fit** lines the frame up with a sample avatar.
4. Enter a **Title** and a **Description**.
5. For a sticker, enter the **Slug**, which is the name people type between colons, such as `party_cat`. It is 2 to 32 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, can't be all digits, and is locked once the sticker is published. Then set **Uses per purchase** (up to 100) and **Price per extra use**.
6. Set the **Sell price**. The minimum is 500 Buzz. A sticker's price must also cover its uses at 5 Buzz each, so a sticker with 100 uses must cost at least 500 Buzz. The form shows "You earn" for each sale.
7. Optional: **Quantity**, the most that will ever be sold. Leave it empty for no limit.
8. Optional: **Accept Blue Buzz**, and **Let other creators sell this** with a **Reseller's cut**.
9. Pick which Buzz color pays the fee under **Pay fee with**, tick the rights confirmation, and click **Submit for review**.

The rights confirmation says you own the artwork or hold the rights to sell it, and that you accept responsibility for any claim from its sale. Civitai keeps a record of it with your submission.

### The submission fee {#fees}

Submitting costs a fee in Buzz. The form shows the exact amount before you pay. The amount is set by Civitai, can differ by item type, and can change, so always read the number on the form. A pack has its own, separate fee. The fee is charged when you submit and is **not refunded** if your item is rejected. If Civitai asks for changes, you can revise and resubmit at no extra cost.

### Review {#review}

An item goes through these states, which you can filter on the Manage Your Shop page: Draft, Pending Review, Changes Requested, Published, Rejected and Archived.

- **Pending Review**: a moderator is checking it. A cosmetic you made can't be worn, even by you, until it is approved.
- **Changes Requested**: you can edit and resubmit.
- **Rejected**: final. A rejected item can't be edited.
- **Published**: on sale. You receive your own copy of the item. For a sticker, you also receive ten times the uses a buyer gets, for your own use.

If a moderator rejects an item that has already sold, or takes it down later for a terms violation, its buyers are refunded automatically and the refund comes out of what you were paid for it.

### Editing and delisting {#editing}

- On a published item you can change the price, the quantity, **Accept Blue Buzz**, the resale terms and, for a sticker, **Uses per purchase** and **Price per extra use**. The title, description, artwork and a sticker's slug are locked.
- Changing a published price by more than 25% from the last approved price sends the item back to review, which takes it off sale until it is approved again.
- Artwork can't be changed once an item has sold.
- In the item's menu, **Delist** takes a published item off sale without deleting it (the same menu then says **List**), and **Archive** retires it (then **Restore**). An item that comes back from either goes through review again. Delisting or archiving also ends every resale listing of your item, and removes it from your featured items. A changes-requested item shows **Edit & resubmit**.

### Building packs {#building-packs}

Use **Build a pack** on the Manage Your Shop page. A pack needs a **Pack name**, between 2 and 20 items chosen from published items, a **Pack price** and an optional cover image and quantity. The form shows the minimum under Pack price, and the button is **Submit pack**. A pack can accept Blue Buzz only if every item in it does. The minimum price: it must be at least 500 Buzz per item, and items made by other creators must be covered at their own list price so a pack can't undercut them. Packs are reviewed like items, with their own separate fee.

### Letting others sell your item {#resale}

When you tick **Let other creators sell this**, other creators can list your item in their own shops. You set a **Reseller's cut**, from 0 to 70 percent of the sale price, taken out of your share. Sales through a reseller use your price and your stock. A reseller keeps the cut they listed under, even if you change it later. You can see who resells your items in the Manage Your Shop table. To list someone else's item, use **Resell a cosmetic**.

### How you are paid {#how-you-are-paid}

You keep **70%** of each sale, rounded down, and Civitai keeps the rest. A reseller's cut comes out of your 70%. You are paid in the Buzz color the buyer used. Blue Buzz you earn can't be cashed out. The Manage Your Shop page shows Published, Pending review, Units sold, Revenue and what you keep.

Sticker top-ups pay you the same way: 70% of each use you sold.

### Shop settings {#shop-settings}

**Shop settings** on the Manage Your Shop page controls:

- **Visibility**: a new shop is a draft that only you can see. **Publish shop** on the draft banner, or the **Shop is public** switch followed by **Save changes**, makes it visible to visitors. You can only publish once the shop has at least one item.
- **Featured cosmetics**: pick up to 6 published items to show at the top.
- **Description**: a short text about your shop.
- Which sections show, such as items from other creators.

## A worked example {#worked-example}

You draw a ghost and want to sell it as a sticker.

1. Export a 400 x 400 px PNG with a transparent background. That is within 320 to 512 px and 1:1.
2. In Submit an item, choose Sticker, upload the file, name it "Shy Ghost", and enter the slug `shy_ghost`.
3. Set Uses per purchase to 100 and Price per extra use to 5. Set Sell price to 600, which is above both the 500 minimum and the 500 needed for 100 uses at 5 each. The form shows that you earn 420 Buzz per sale (70% of 600).
4. Leave Quantity empty, turn on Accept Blue Buzz, pick which color pays the fee, tick the rights box, and submit.
5. Moderators approve it. Open Manage Your Shop and press **Publish shop** in the draft banner, or open Shop settings, turn on **Shop is public** and click **Save changes**. You can publish while the sticker is still in review. Visitors, and the main shop, see it once it is approved. The main shop only lists items from public shops. You get your own copy, with 1,000 uses.

A buyer on civitai.com pays 600 Buzz and gets 100 uses. They put the sticker in 30 comments and have 70 uses left. Later they run out and buy 20 more uses for 100 Buzz (20 x 5). You earn 70 Buzz from that top-up. Compare the fee shown on the form with the 420 Buzz you earn per sale to see how many sales it takes to earn the fee back.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Artwork does not meet the requirements for this cosmetic type" | One of the automatic checks failed. The form lists each check. Common causes are no transparent background, a wrong size or ratio, or a file that isn't PNG or WebP. |
| "This artwork has already been submitted to the shop." | The same image was submitted before. Submit your own original artwork. |
| "`N` uses needs at least `X` Buzz" | A sticker's price has to cover 5 Buzz per use. Raise the price or lower the uses. |
| "A single use must cost at least 5 Buzz" | Raise the price per extra use. |
| Your Buzz balance doesn't cover the fee | Pay the fee from a different color, or buy more Buzz. The fee is shown beside each balance. |
| "Published items can only change price and quantity" | Title, description and artwork are locked once an item is published. |
| "This item does not accept Blue Buzz" | The creator did not turn on Blue Buzz for it. Pay with your site's color. |
| "This `item` is out of stock", "not available yet" or "no longer available" | A quantity limit or a sale window applies. |
| "You don't have enough uses left on one of these stickers. Remove it or buy more." | A sticker in your comment has no uses left. Buy more uses, or take it out. |
| Visitors can't see your Shop tab | A shop stays hidden from visitors until you publish it. You always see it yourself. Open Manage Your Shop and use **Publish shop**. |
| A sticker can't be placed on an image | The owner has turned stickers off, or the sticker is limited by rating and the image doesn't match or is unrated. You may also have used your free placement for the day, which resets at midnight UTC, or you already have 10 placements waiting with that creator, or the image is your own. |

## I need more help {#help}

If something here does not match what you see, a purchase went wrong, or a review decision is unclear, contact Civitai through the [support portal](https://civitai.com/support). For how Buzz colors work, see [Buzz Colors: Blue, Yellow and Green](/buzz-colors-blue-yellow-and-green/). To earn from your models as well, see [Civitai's Guide to Earning with the Creator Program](/civitais-guide-to-earning-with-the-creator-program/).
