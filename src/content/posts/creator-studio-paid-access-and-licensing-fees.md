This guide is for creators who want to charge for a model: either by selling access to it, or by earning a small fee every time someone generates with it. It covers where you set each option, the limits you will run into, and where the Buzz you earn shows up.

It replaces [Civitai's Guide to Early Access](/civitais-guide-to-early-access/), which describes the 2024 version of the feature. The other half of "licensing" on Civitai, the permissions you set for how people may use a model, has its own page: [Guide to Civitai Model Licensing Options](/guide-to-licensing-options-on-civitai/).

## What Creator Studio is {#what-creator-studio-is}

Creator Studio is a separate site for creators at [creator-studio.civitai.com](https://creator-studio.civitai.com). You sign in with your Civitai account. It brings three jobs into one place:

- **Pricing your models.** Set licensing fees and paid access on one version, or on many versions at once.
- **Watching usage and earnings.** See generations, downloads, reactions and earnings for each model and each base model.
- **Cashing out.** Link through to the Creator Program, which turns Buzz you have earned into money.

![The Creator Studio landing page, showing three sections: Licensing fees, Real usage analytics and Creator Program payouts](/images/guides/creator-studio-paid-access-and-licensing-fees/creator-studio-landing.webp)

Once you are signed in, the sidebar has these sections:

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | A starting page with links into everything else |
| Monetization, then Models | Your model versions, with fees and paid access settings, filters and bulk edits |
| Earnings | Your earnings broken down by source |
| Analytics | Overview, Models, Base models, Engagement, Content, Audience and Storage |
| Settings | Payout status, membership and defaults |

If you see a **member** badge next to Monetization and you are not in the Creator Program, you can still set prices. The badge is only a hint. Cashing out earnings is a different step, covered under [How you earn](#how-you-earn).

You can also set the same options one version at a time from the version's edit page on civitai.com. Creator Studio is the faster route once you have more than a few models, because you can filter your catalog, see which versions already carry a price, and change several at once.

:::note
Creator Studio may show extra pages on some accounts while new tools are tested. This guide covers the sections every account sees.
:::

## The three things you can charge for {#three-things}

Civitai has three separate pricing tools. People mix them up, so here they are side by side.

| | Early Access | Paid Access | Licensing fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who pays | A buyer, once | A buyer, once | Anyone who generates with your model, each time |
| How long | A set number of days, then the version is free | Never ends | Stays until you change it |
| What it unlocks | Download and on-site generation (depending on your settings) | Same | Nothing is locked. The fee is added to the cost of a generation |
| Counts against your monthly limit | No | Yes, one slot | Yes, one slot |

A version can carry a fee, a gate, both, or neither. A gate is the general name for Early Access and Paid Access: whichever one you choose, the version sits behind a purchase.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

A few rules decide whether you can set a price at all.

- **Creator score.** Setting a new fee or a new gate needs a creator score of at least 10,000. This is the score shown on your account settings page, at [civitai.com/user/account](https://civitai.com/user/account).
- **Base model.** A version built on a base model that is licensed for non-commercial use only cannot carry a fee or a paid gate. The version edit page tells you when this applies.
- **Likeness models.** Models flagged as depicting a real person cannot be put behind paid access or carry a licensing fee.
- **Rights affirmation.** The first time you charge for a version, you confirm that you have the right to do so. You only need to do this once per version. You never have to confirm anything to remove a price.

## Early Access: a timed window {#early-access}

Early Access puts a new version behind a purchase for a set number of days. When the window ends, the version becomes free for everyone.

Rules that matter:

- **You start it before the first publish.** A timed window can only be started on a version that has never been published. After that, Creator Studio shows "Early access can't be started after a version is published." Plan the window when you create the version, not after the release.
- **How long it can be.** On civitai.com you choose from 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15 or 30 days. In Creator Studio you type the number of days, up to your maximum. The longest window you can choose grows with your creator score:

| Creator score | Longest window | Versions in Early Access at once |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10,000 | 3 days | 1 |
| 65,000 | 5 days | 2 |
| 90,000 | 7 days | 4 |
| 125,000 | 9 days | 6 |
| 200,000 | 12 days | 8 |
| 250,000 | 15 days | 20 |

  A 30-day window is only available to accounts that have been granted it. These thresholds are the current values in the Civitai app and can change.
- **Membership does not unlock it.** The ladder reads your creator score only.
- **Turning it off.** Setting the duration to 0 and saving turns Early Access off. Once a published version has had its window removed, it cannot be started again.
- **Abuse.** Taking a version out of Early Access by unpublishing it and republishing it to start a new window is treated as abuse of the feature.

## Paid Access: permanent {#paid-access}

Paid Access never ends. Buyers always pay to unlock the version.

- You can add it at any time, including to a version that is already published.
- It uses one of your monthly pricing slots (see below).
- Switching a version from Early Access to Paid Access is allowed. Going the other way on a published version is not, because a timed window cannot be started after publishing.

## Setting it up in Creator Studio {#setting-it-up}

1. Open [creator-studio.civitai.com](https://creator-studio.civitai.com) and sign in.
2. In the sidebar, open **Monetization**, then **Models**.
3. Find your model. You can search by name and filter by model type, base model, or "Has early / paid access".
4. Select the version. The side panel has a section called **Early & paid access**.
5. Choose the mode: **Early Access** (timed) or **Paid Access** (permanent). Early Access shows a field for the number of days.
6. Fill in **Price for access**, in Buzz. The lowest access price is 100 Buzz. There is no upper limit.
7. Choose how generation works for buyers (see the next section).
8. Save with **Save early access** or **Save paid access**. To remove a gate, use **Turn off early access** or **Turn off paid access**.

The same section also has **How this version can be used**, which decides whether buyers get the files or on-site generation only. Generation-only is available to some accounts only, and the panel says so when it is not available to yours.

On civitai.com the same choices are in the version edit form, under **Charge for access to this version**, with an **Access mode** switch between **Early Access (timed)** and **Paid Access (permanent)**.

### Generation, free previews and Blue Buzz {#generation-options}

A gate covers the download and on-site generation. For generation you pick one of three options:

- **Same as the access price.** One price unlocks both.
- **A cheaper generation-only price.** Buyers who only want to generate pay less. It must be at least 50 Buzz and cannot be higher than the access price.
- **Free for everyone.** Anyone can generate on-site without buying. Only the download is gated.

You can also set **Free preview generations**, a number of test generations people get before they have to buy. It starts at 10 and can go up to 1,000. Leave it empty or set 0 for none.

**Also accept Blue Buzz** lets buyers pay the same price with Blue Buzz. You are paid in Blue Buzz for those purchases. Blue Buzz can be spent on generation but cannot be withdrawn or converted to cash.

### Donation goals {#donation-goals}

On a timed window you can turn on **Let the community unlock this early**. You set a goal amount in Buzz. If purchases and donations reach the goal before the window ends, Early Access ends and the version becomes free. Purchases made with Yellow or Green Buzz count toward the goal. Blue Buzz purchases do not.

A goal can only be created once. After it is set, Creator Studio shows it but cannot change it. A permanent gate has no goal.

## Licensing fees {#licensing-fees}

A licensing fee is a small amount of Buzz you earn each time someone generates with your version on Civitai. It does not lock anything. It is added to the cost of the generation.

### Setting a fee in Creator Studio {#setting-a-fee}

1. Open **Monetization**, then **Models**, and select the version.
2. In the side panel, find the section titled **Licensing fee**, above **Early & paid access**. Its field reads "⚡ per" and then a number of "generations".
3. Enter the Buzz amount and choose the number of generations from the list. If you are unsure, click **Use this** next to the "Suggested" line to fill in the suggested value.
4. Select **Save fee**. The first time you charge for a version, tick the rights confirmation first. This section saves separately from Early & paid access.

On civitai.com, open the version edit form. Turn on **I want to monetize this version** and confirm the rights statement. Then turn on **Charge a fee to generate with this version**, and set the fee under **Fee per generation** (a Buzz amount, then the number of generations).

### The fee ratio {#fee-ratio}

You do not type a decimal. You set a ratio: some amount of Buzz for some number of generations, such as **1 Buzz per 10 generations**. The options for the number of generations are 1, 10, 20, 50 and 100. Leave the field empty to clear the fee.

Some examples:

| You enter | Fee per generation |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Buzz per 10 generations | 0.1 Buzz |
| 1 Buzz per 1 generation | 1 Buzz |
| 5 Buzz per 50 generations | 0.1 Buzz |
| 50 Buzz per 1 generation | 50 Buzz |

Limits and defaults:

- **The ceiling is 100 Buzz per generation** for image models and 500 Buzz per generation for video models. It is the same for every creator, whatever your membership.
- **A suggested starting value** is offered for a new version: 1 Buzz per 10 generations for most model types, and 1 Buzz per generation for checkpoints. Video models start at five times those values. Creator Studio shows it as a "Suggested" line with a **Use this** link. The version edit form on civitai.com fills it in for you.
- **The smallest fee is 1 Buzz per 100 generations.** Anything that rounds to less is refused.
- **The ceiling only blocks raises.** A fee you set earlier that is above the current ceiling keeps working, and unrelated edits do not lower it.

### Fees inherited from the base model {#inherited-fees}

Some base models carry a licensing fee of their own. If your version is a fine-tune of one of them, the version edit form on civitai.com asks you to pick the parent in a field called **Fine-tuned from**, with a default already selected. The help text there says the parent's fee applies to generations made with your model.

Two things follow:

- **Your fee is added on top.** If you also set your own fee, the form says yours is added to the parent's.
- **The parent's share goes to the parent's creator.** It is not yours, and it does not stop you earning your own fee.

The picker only lists parents that have been registered for this, and only appears for versions where it applies. If you see no **Fine-tuned from** field, there is nothing to inherit.

### A worked example {#worked-example}

You publish a LoRA and set a fee of 1 Buzz per 10 generations, plus Paid Access at 100 Buzz.

- Someone buys access at 100 Buzz: you receive 100 Buzz, in the kind of Buzz they paid with.
- People generate with your LoRA 25 times in a day. The fee is 0.1 Buzz per generation, so the fee adds up to about 2.5 Buzz.
- The version used one pricing slot, because it carries a price. Editing either price later uses none.
- On those generations you earn the fee instead of creator compensation.

The fee is charged per generation, so what you earn depends on use.

## How many prices you can set: pricing slots {#pricing-slots}

Membership does not change how much you can charge. It changes how many versions you can newly price each month.

A **price** is a licensing fee or a permanent paid access gate. Timed Early Access is not counted. Each version uses one slot for the month, however many prices it carries.

| Membership tier | New priced versions per month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 3 |
| Bronze | 10 |
| Silver | 25 |
| Gold | Unlimited |

Rules for slots:

- **Editing is free.** Changing, lowering or clearing a price you have already set uses no slot.
- **Clearing can give a slot back.** If you remove the last price from a version nobody has used or bought, and you set it in the current month, the slot is returned.
- **A lapsed membership lowers the allowance right away.** Your allowance drops to the Free amount, but prices you already set keep working.
- **Creator Studio shows what you used.** The Models page has a counter, and you can see which versions spent your slots.

## Bulk editing {#bulk-editing}

On the Models page you can select several versions and apply a fee, a gate, or a usage setting to all of them. If any selected version has not had a rights affirmation, you are asked once for the whole batch. A published version cannot start a timed window, so the dialog counts those as skipped and tells you that permanent paid access still works for them.

## How you earn {#how-you-earn}

Buzz reaches you through six channels, which appear as columns on the **Analytics, Models** page and on each model's page:

| Channel | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| License Fees | Your per-generation fee |
| Compensation | Creator compensation for generations with your models |
| Generation Tips | Tips from people who generated with your model |
| Early Access | Purchases of a timed window |
| Perm. Access | Purchases of permanent access |
| Donations | Donations to a goal |

The **Earnings** page shows the same money by source. The Generation Tips column only appears once you have received one.

Points to know:

- **You receive the price the buyer pays.** When someone buys access, the Buzz they spend is paid to you, after any sale discount, in the same kind of Buzz they paid with.
- **A fee replaces creator compensation on those generations.** If your version charges a licensing fee, a generation with it earns you the fee instead of creator compensation. A fee you inherit from a base model goes to the parent's creator and does not switch yours off.
- **Cashing out is a separate step.** Yellow Buzz you have earned can be banked in the Creator Program and withdrawn as money. See [Civitai's Guide to Earning with the Creator Program](/civitais-guide-to-earning-with-the-creator-program/) for the requirements, the banking caps and the payout process. Creator Studio's Settings page shows your payout status.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Early access can't be started after a version is published" | Timed windows must start before the first publish. Use Paid Access, or plan the window for your next version. |
| "Early access isn't available for your account yet" | Your creator score is below 10,000. The sentence after it says whether paid access is blocked by your score or by this month's pricing limit. |
| You can't choose Paid Access | Your creator score is below 10,000, or you have used this month's pricing slots. Free up a slot by clearing a price you no longer need, or wait for the month to turn. |
| "Paid access isn't available for this version" | The version is set to API-only generation. It has no download or on-site generation mode to sell. |
| Paid access is blocked for a version | Check the base model license, whether the model is flagged as depicting a real person, and whether the model is private. Private models can still carry a fee. |

If something here does not match what you see, or a limit is blocking you and the reason is not clear, contact Civitai through the [support portal](https://civitai.com/support).
