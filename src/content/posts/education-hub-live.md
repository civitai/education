**Introducing the Civitai Education Hub: Your Gateway to Generative AI Mastery!**

Hey Civitans! Ready to dive into the fascinating world of generative AI art?

Our brand new **Education Hub** is now live, designed specifically to fuel your passion and creativity. Whether you’re just starting out with Generative AI art tools or looking to level up your skills, you’ll find a treasure trove of tutorials, guides, and resources here.

Currently, we’ve got the amazing [Generative AI Glossary](//generative-ai-glossary/) – an ongoing project to list and explain every Generative AI term imaginable! We’re also discussing the basics of [using](//sdxl-1-0/) and [training SDXL](//sdxl-1-0-training-overview/).

Over the next few weeks we’ll be rapidly expanding our articles list, delving into topics such as Basic & Advanced Prompting, ControlNet, and LoRA Training!
