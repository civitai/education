## What's new in v2 {#v2}

- **A new home.** The game now lives at [knights.civitai.com](https://knights.civitai.com). You sign in with your Civitai account. The old Civitai games page for Knights of New Order sends you there.
- **A gold shop.** Gold no longer turns into Buzz automatically. You spend it in a fixed shop instead.
- **A new rank.** Paladin sits above Templar.
- **The Watch.** A daily habit that pays gold for finishing 30 judgments.
- **A rolling weekly leaderboard.**

## Knights of What? {#what}

**Knights of New Order** is a game where you rate images submitted by other users. You judge each one against Civitai's community standards, and your ratings help decide what an image's content rating should be. In return you earn XP, gold and rank.

**Sensitive content warning.** Knights of New Order shows sensitive and explicit imagery, regardless of your on-site browsing level settings. It may not be suitable for everyone. You see a warning that you must accept before you enter the game.

The game has no bearing on whether an image is visible on the site, and it is separate from Civitai's automatic image scanning and tagging.

## Getting in {#join}

Go to [knights.civitai.com](https://knights.civitai.com) and sign in with your Civitai account. Accounts that are banned cannot enter, and muted accounts cannot judge.

## Ranks {#ranks}

| Rank | How you get there | Gold multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acolyte | Everyone starts here. Rate images and learn. | n/a |
| Knight | Level 20 and 70% accuracy | 1x |
| Templar | Level 50 and 80% accuracy | 1.25x |
| Paladin | Level 90, 85% accuracy and 60 days played | 1.5x |

Accuracy is measured over your last 500 graded votes. If your accuracy falls below the bar for your rank, your gold and your vote weight are both halved until it recovers.

### Acolyte (training rank) {#acolyte}

All players begin here. An Acolyte learns through an apprenticeship: a steady run of images that have a known answer, so you get feedback as you go. You earn 100 gold for each level you gain, up to level 20. At level 20, with enough accuracy, you become a Knight.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Acolyte.webp)

### Knight {#knight}

Knights judge real images. Each accurate rating earns gold, and you can be smitten for judgments far from the correct outcome (see [Smites](#smites)).

![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Knight.webp)

### Templar {#templar}

Templars settle cases that Knights cannot agree on.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Templar.webp)

### Paladin {#paladin}

Paladin is the newest rank, and it is the highest rank a player can reach. It asks for the most: level 90, 85% accuracy and 60 days played.

## Gold and the shop {#gold}

Gold no longer converts to Buzz on its own. You spend it in the shop at knights.civitai.com. The shop sells:

- **Buzz packs.** The rate is 20 gold for 1 Buzz.
- **Knights cosmetics.** Some need a certain rank. They show on your Civitai profile, in your comments and on your avatar.
- **Membership weeks** (Bronze, Silver or Gold), for people who are not already members.

## The Watch {#watch}

Complete 30 judgments in a day to finish that day's Watch. Finishing it pays 100 gold per day of your streak, up to 7 days. You get one free shield a week to protect a streak. Keep a streak going for 7, 30 and 100 days to earn Vigil badges.

## The leaderboard {#leaderboard}

The leaderboard is weekly, counted over the last 7 days, and ranks players by **fervor**, a measure of how accurate and consistent your ratings are.

## Smites {#smites}

- You are smitten when a rating is far from the correct outcome. Rating explicit content as safe is penalized right away.
- Smites reduce your vote weight, so penalized players have less say until they show accuracy over time.
- Accounts with suspicious voting patterns, such as the same rating for every image, are reset automatically.
- There are limits on how fast and how many ratings you can submit. Vote at a natural pace and you will not notice them.

## How images are judged {#judging}

Several Knights vote independently on an image. If they cannot agree, or the result is far from the system's own estimate, the image goes to Templars, then to Paladins, and finally to site moderators, whose decision is final.

## Moving from v1 {#carry-over}

- **Carried over:** your rank, XP, badges and cosmetics.
- **Starts fresh:** gold, streaks, judgment history and the leaderboard.
- **Gold you had pending in v1** is paid out as Buzz.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Do I lose cosmetics if I drop a rank?**
No. You keep the cosmetics you earned.

**Which images are rated?**
Published images are sent into the rating queue.
