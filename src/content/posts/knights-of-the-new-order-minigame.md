## Recent Changes

**April 2026 Update – Fairness & Anti-Abuse Improvements**

We’ve made several changes to ensure the Rating Game remains fair and rewarding for honest players:

-   **Fervor now rewards accuracy** – Fervor is now weighted by your accuracy ratio. High-accuracy players will see little change, but low-accuracy players will earn significantly less Fervor.
-   **Voting speed limits** – Limits on how fast and how many ratings you can submit per minute, hour, and day. Vote at a natural pace and you won’t notice them.
-   **Stronger sanity check penalties** – Failing sanity checks carries heavier consequences. Severe under-ratings (e.g., rating explicit content as safe) result in immediate penalties. Repeat failures within 24 hours escalate further.
-   **Automatic account resets** – Accounts with suspicious voting patterns (e.g., spamming the same rating for everything) will be automatically reset.
-   **Vote weight affected by penalties** – Smites now reduce your vote weight, meaning penalized players have less influence on rating outcomes until they prove accuracy over time.

## Gameplay

### **Image Rating**

Each participant is asked to assess user-submitted images based on existing site guidelines. Ratings determine whether content is appropriate, and help shape moderation outcomes.

### **Gold**

-   Awarded to **Knights** and **Templars** for each accurate rating.
-   Every correct rating equals 100 gold.
-   1000 gold equals 1 Yellow Buzz.
-   Gold enters a **3-day holding period**, after which it is converted into **Yellow** **Buzz**, [our](/civitais-guide-to-on-site-currency-buzz-%e2%9a%a1/) [site](/civitais-guide-to-on-site-currency-buzz-%e2%9a%a1/) [currency](/civitais-guide-to-on-site-currency-buzz-%e2%9a%a1/).

### **Fervor**

-   Measures your **accuracy** over time. Fervor is now **weighted by your accuracy ratio** – the more accurate you are, the more Fervor you earn.
-   The 12 Knights with the **highest weekly Fervor** are promoted to **Templar** status.
-   Fervor resets weekly, encouraging your ongoing participation and precision!

### **Smites**

-   Issued when your rating diverges _significantly_ from the correct outcome. Severe under-ratings (e.g., rating explicit content as safe) result in **immediate penalties**.
-   Accumulate 3 Smites and you’re **demoted back to Level 1** – a fall from grace.
-   Can be cleansed:
    -   Automatically after 7 days of faithful service
    -   By levelling up
    -   By continued correct judgments
    -   Through Inquisitor intervention
-   Smites now **reduce your vote weight**, meaning penalized players have less influence on rating outcomes until they demonstrate accuracy over time.

### **The Hierarchy of Divine Judgement**

Content is judged through a sacred chain of command:

#### The Knights

-   **5 Knights** must independently vote and reach **a unanimous agreement** on an image’s rating.

-   If consensus cannot be reached, or if the community rating significantly **deviates from the system’s estimated rating**, the image is escalated.

#### The Templar Council

The next tier of judgement.

-   **2 Templars** are summoned to review the case.
-   If **both agree**, the rating is finalized.
-   If they **disagree**, the image is ascended to the highest authority.

#### The Inquisition

Composed of **site Moderators**, the Inquisition delivers the final verdict.

-   Their decision is **absolute**.
-   No further appeals. No higher court. Only righteous judgement.

## FAQ

**Q**: **I’m not reading all that! What is Knights of New Order?**
**A**: It’s an expansion of the old Image Rating Game – a gamified site moderation tool where you rate images to help enforce content standards, climb ranks, and earn rewards like Buzz and exclusive cosmetics.

**Q**: **What happens if I drop out of a rank – do I lose cosmetics?**
**A**: No! You’ll retain your earned cosmetic badges!

**Q**: **Is there a daily cap on Gold/Earnings?**
**A**: No, at present there is no maximum Gold earning cap. This may be adjusted as we monitor usage.

**Q**: **Which images are being rated?**
**A**: All Published images are sent into the rating queue. PG and PG-13 content will be reviewed first, but if that image queue is empty, all levels of content may be shown.

**Note**: the Knights of New Order rating game has no bearing on image visibility on-site, and is distinct from our image ingestion and tagging services.
