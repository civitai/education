Every model can have a card for each site. One is the card for **civitai.red**, and one is the card for **civitai.com**. Each has its own **title**, **description** and **cover**, so you can present your model to the audience of each site. It is still one model, with the same files, versions and downloads on both.

## What you get {#what-you-get}

- On **civitai.com**, people see your civitai.com card.
- On **civitai.red**, people who have mature content turned on see your civitai.red card.
- Until you set up a civitai.com card, both sites show the same card.

You set the two cards up in the model editor, in the **civitai.com** and **civitai.red** tabs. You do not upload a second model.

## Who sees which card {#who-sees-what}

The card follows the viewer's browsing level, not only the site they are on.

| Viewer | Sees |
|---|---|
| Anyone on civitai.com, signed in or not | The civitai.com card |
| Anyone signed out on civitai.red | The civitai.com card |
| Someone on civitai.red with mature content turned off | The civitai.com card |
| Someone on civitai.red with mature content turned on | The civitai.red card |

You and moderators can open both cards in the editor.

## What a card sets {#what-it-sets}

Only three things can differ between the two cards: the **title**, the **description** and the **cover**. Everything else about the model is shared, such as its files, versions, type, tags and permissions.

While a model has its own civitai.com card, civitai.com does not show its version descriptions. Version names and trigger words are shown as they are, so keep them suitable for both sites.

## Set it up {#set-it-up}

1. Open the model form. For a model you already have, open the model page, choose the **⋮** button (**Model options**) at the top right of the title, then **Edit Model**. The page is headed **Editing model**. The civitai.com tab is in the same form in the first step of the upload wizard, but the cover picker only appears once the model exists.
2. In the first box, **Title, description & cover**, you will see two tabs, **civitai.red** and **civitai.com**. The civitai.red tab holds your original title, description and cover.
3. Open the **civitai.com** tab. Until you write something, it says civitai.com shows the same title and description as civitai.red, and the tab carries a **Same as .red** badge.
4. Choose **Write a civitai.com version** to start from a copy of your current text, or **Draft a civitai.com version** to have a version written for you from your current text. The draft is only a suggestion. Nothing is saved until you save the model. If you already have different civitai.com text, you are asked before the draft replaces it.
5. Edit the **Title** and the **Description**. A line above each field checks the text as you type and tells you if it is not suitable for civitai.com. Leave the description empty if you want civitai.com to show none. The civitai.red description is never shown there instead.
6. Pick a **Cover** for civitai.com. Only your own images rated PG or PG-13, published on this model, are offered. Click the picked image again to clear it. The picker appears once the model exists. If it says none of your images are rated PG or PG-13 yet, or asks you to publish a post with images on the model, do that first.
7. Choose the blue **Save** button at the bottom of the page. You return to the model page. As soon as the tab holds your own text (after step 4), it shows an **Own content** badge instead of **Same as .red**.

To go back, choose **Use the civitai.red listing** in the civitai.com tab and confirm. The civitai.com title, description and cover are removed, and civitai.com shows the same card as civitai.red.

## Mature models {#mature-models}

civitai.com shows only PG and PG-13 content. If a model is marked mature, it is not listed on civitai.com unless it has a civitai.com card. Setting up that card is how a mature model gets a listing there. The model stays mature, and civitai.red keeps its standing and its card.

A mature model is listed on civitai.com once its civitai.com card is complete. That means:

- a civitai.com **title**, and
- a civitai.com **cover**: one of your own images, rated PG or PG-13, that is scanned, not flagged, and sits on a published post on a published version of the model.

The description is optional. Visitors on civitai.com see only the model's PG and PG-13 images.

If the cover stops qualifying, for example the image or post is deleted, moderated or re-rated, or the version is unpublished, the model leaves civitai.com again. Pick a new cover to bring it back.

## Checks and review {#review}

Civitai checks the civitai.com title and description with the same text scan that helps decide whether a model is mature. Each field shows **Suitable for civitai.com**, or a line such as "This isn't suitable for civitai.com" with its rating and a short reason. The check runs again when you save. If a title or description is rated above PG-13, a red message says **Failed to save model** and that the civitai.com title or description can't be shown on civitai.com. Nothing is saved, so edit it and save again. If the check cannot run, you are asked to try saving again. No one has to approve your civitai.com card before it goes up.

## Questions {#faq}

**Do I need to upload the model twice?**
No. It is one model with two cards.

**What can differ between the two cards?**
Only the title, the description and the cover. Everything else is shared.

**Why is my mature model not on civitai.com yet?**
It needs a civitai.com title and a qualifying PG or PG-13 cover. Check the civitai.com tab for both.

**What do visitors on civitai.com see if a mature model has a civitai.com title but no cover?**
The model is not listed there yet. A visitor who opens its page sees a note that mature content now lives on civitai.red. Add a qualifying cover to list it.

**Does the civitai.com card change the mature mark?**
No. The model stays mature and keeps its standing on civitai.red.

**Why does the model not show up in the generator on civitai.com?**
Version levels are not changed. Mature versions stay out of safe-for-work places such as the generator's resource picker on civitai.com.
