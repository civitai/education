## The Basics

### What is Civitai?

[Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) is a thriving hub of Generative AI resource sharing, offering a platform where users can not only discover, download, and share content, but also review and discuss a diverse range of resources related to Stable Diffusion, and other Generative AI technologies.

Like a digital library, the website encompasses a broad range of content types, spanning an extensive range of categories, accommodating the interests of a wide audience. Whether you’re a fan of anime characters, a celebrity enthusiast, DnD fantasy fan, a photography hobbyist, a fan of cartoons, and beyond – [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) curates a collection of resources that caters to practically unlimited preferences.

As an alternative to Huggingface, [Civitai.com](http://civitai.com/) is more than just a means of storing models; it’s a vibrant community contributing towards the evolution of Stable Diffusion, and Generative AI.

### Why’s it called Civitai? {#why-s-it-called-civitai}

Contrary to popular belief Civitai has nothing to do with the **Civet** – an animal known for consuming and excreting valuable coffee beans. Civit comes from the Latin word ‘Civitas’, meaning **community**.

You can read more about [our naming origins here](https://civitai.com/articles/897/the-tale-of-civitai-from-model-share-to-millions).

### The Civitai Terms of Service (ToS)

Whenever you’re uploading content, whether it’s a model, a review, or an image post, you must keep the Terms of Service in mind; [https://civitai.com/content/tos](https://civitai.com/content/tos). Please review the **Content Removal Criteria**, prior to sharing content to Civitai.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Screenshot-2025-07-30-174108.webp)

Some of the rules _**most commonly violated**_ by Content Creators are;

-   **Non-AI Content** – We don’t allow any real photographs of people, places, or objects on Civitai.
-   **Photorealistic Depictions of Minors** – Models intended for the photorealistic depiction of minors are not permitted. Additionally, _any_ photorealistic image of a minor is not permitted.
-   **Other Minors** – Non-Photorealistic images of minors are acceptable, providing they are not posed in a sexually suggestive or inappropriate way.

Inappropriate content is reviewed and removed at the moderator’s discretion.

If you believe your content has been removed unfairly, you can submit an appeal through our on-site process for further review **by a team separate from the moderation team**.

### How is Content Moderated?

Every uploaded image undergoes AI content and moderation tagging (utilizing a number of third-party partner services) to automatically flag potentially inappropriate content. If a model is marked as representing a minor and the image contains either inappropriate content or is photorealistic, **it’s reviewed by a human moderator before being allowed onto the site.** Our AI detection isn’t perfect, so if you spot something, please report it.

Minor content undergoes additional checks which can be read about in our [Content Rules: Minors policy](https://civitai.com/content/rules/minors).

Similarly, content that depicts, or is based on the likeness of real people – living or deceased – including public figures, celebrities, influencers, and private individuals, is strictly prohibited.

### Reporting Site Content

All uploaded content can be manually flagged for review, including user accounts, models, model sample images, reviews, review images, and comments. We encourage the Community to help the moderation team police the site, **by reporting resources which violate the Terms of Service**.

There are many ways to report content, specific to each content type. Details are included in the relevant sections, below.

## Navigating the Site

The website, accessible via [Civitai.com](http://Civitai.com), [Civit.ai](http://Civit.ai), or [Civitai.green](https://civitai.green/) (for our SFW only site), is designed to be intuitive and easy to navigate, but there are many site sections, and numerous features which may not be immediately apparent to first-time visitors!

This guide will explain everything necessary to get the most out of Civitai, from posting images, reviews, and engaging with creators to uploading your own resources, and attaining a coveted Leaderboard badge!

### Creating an Account

The site can be browsed, and safe-for-work content displayed and downloaded, _**without an account**_.

If you want access to mature content (18+ only content) and other site features, you must **create an account**, and _**specifically opt-in to adult content**_.

Creating an account is as simple as clicking the **Sign In** button at the top right of every page.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Screenshot-2024-01-25-125246-1024x45.webp)

You can sign into Civitai with a **Discord**, **Github**, **Google**, or **Reddit** account, or an **email address.**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-44.webp)

Follow the steps to create your account – it’s really simple, but if you run into problems you can email [support@civitai.com](mailto:support@civitai.com) for help!

#### Content Moderation Settings

One of the greatest things about Civitai is its diversity of content – there’s something for everyone. However, some people might not want to see certain categories of content, and we’ve designed the site to be **_extremely_** granular in the types of content that can be filtered out.

The **Hidden Tags** field allows resources with specific tags to be completely hidden from view, e.g. anime. Use the search box to find and exclude specific content tags from your site experience.

The **Hidden Users** field allows content from _**specific creators**_ to be hidden. Use the search box to find and exclude specific users from your site experience.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-48.webp)
